# What's new, day by day

The short story of Around Warsaw, one day at a time. The website's progress page
(https://aroundwarsaw.app/progress/) and the design wiki both show this file. The full detail of every
update is in docs/ROADMAP.md, under "Progress log", and the known issues are in docs/BACKLOG.md.

How to keep it current: when you add a row to ROADMAP's progress log, also add one short line under
that day here, in plain words a player would understand (no file names, no spoilers). A day that has
rows in the progress log but no section here is listed automatically from the log's labels until
someone writes its lines.

## 2026-10-31
- Złote Tarasy is rebuilt as one landmark: the golden glass wave over the terraced mall, the Lumen offices curving round it, the Skylight tower with a crown that glows at night, and the round glass drum by the station.
- Plac Małachowskiego and the Presidential Palace are real buildings now: Holy Trinity's white rotunda with its four columned porches, green dome and tall lantern; Zachęta in white with its two big columns, sculpted pediment, steps and lamps; the Presidential Palace's wings round the forecourt, a columned portico, Prince Poniatowski on horseback, and four stone lions at the gate.
- The new Museum of Modern Art on Plac Defilad is now its own building: the long white box floating on a glass lobby behind white fins, a thin dark slot round the middle, big windows that glow at night, skylights on the roof, and an info card.
- The towers and the Palace of Culture no longer flicker: overlapping walls and roofs were drawn twice, and now they are drawn once. The look is the same.
- A new, shorter website: the newest screenshots, three missions, open source, a word from the author.
- A new automatic check looks at every landmark model for flickering surfaces, zero-size faces, floating parts and too many triangles, so these get caught before anyone looks at a screenshot.
- Rondo Dmowskiego, the rest of the photos: the city bus is an 18 m yellow-and-red articulated one with a bellows in the middle, the bus shelter has its big lit ad panel, the Nowotel's name has its orange rule and chain line under it, Hotel Metropol's roof carries a green neon name and Rondo 1 has a clinic sign at street level.
- Rondo Dmowskiego at night looks like the photos: the Nowotel's white tower with its name in blue neon, the Rotunda's white zigzag crown with red and blue light, red bus shelters, and yellow trams with a red roof.
- New players get a guided first ten minutes: a hint finger, a first kebab order, a race against Pani Halina, a friendly police caution, a rescue that earns you the helicopter, a tape on the boulevards and Mirek's garage. Win "Trzy mosty" and finish ten jobs for a thank-you card, then drive on.
- The rivals are a ladder now: beat Pani Halina first, earn respect, work up to Mordor Mike, then race Seba over three bridges at night in "Trzy mosty".
- Dusk looks like a real late-October evening in Warsaw: a blue-grey sky, the Palace of Culture lit gold, lit hotel names, the station canopy's lamps and red lights on the towers.
- Twenty little mermaid figurines hide by the kerbs of Warsaw: drive past to find them, with rewards at 5, 10 and 20 and two new paints.
- Warsaw's districts: discover each one, find three "?" in it, and see how much of it you've done on the map. The save keeps a version now, so progress carries into later builds.
- The map grows north: Rondo Radosława, Arkadia and the Forest tower.
- Plac Piłsudskiego is complete, with Hotel Europejski, the Metropolitan, Hotel Wiktoria and the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier.
- Muzeum Narodowe is a real model now.
- Couriers race you at night, and the dumped e-scooters can be rounded up.
- Night buses with the N sign, and buses stop when you sound the horn at a stop.
- A vintage tram, and Warsztat u Mirka: the first garage, with paint jobs.
- Fewer brown blocks on the horizon when you fly the helicopter.
- This page: what's new, day by day.
- The design wiki is online, open to anyone with the link.
- Planned for the lore: morning coffee raves and arthouse cinemas.

## 2026-10-30

- The game is now called Around Warsaw and is open source (MIT); the car radio's main station is KEBAB FM.
- MOJA WARSZAWA, the city notebook: 72 places to find, in 9 chapters.
- The night and the river: club queues, the dawn crowd, the river steps; then dog walkers, runners, cyclists and the café trends.
- PÓŁNOC FM, the late-night station, plus Fotka (a photo mode), KASETA (the time tape) and 45 easter eggs.
- Shop fronts and parody brands, the city guards in the Old Town, wheel clamps and police escorts.
- 40 more landmarks in their real colours: the skyline's towers, the Old Town, the Royal Route, MDM and Sigismund's Column.
- The map engine is faster on the bigger map, and the demo map gets its tunnels and bridges.
- Driving feels less sticky, small props no longer stop the car, and the camera turns with you.
- Rain puddles, night water, store listings and a design wiki.

## 2026-10-29

- Sector streaming: the city loads in pieces as you drive, so it can grow.
- The demo map: 20 km² of central Warsaw, from Rondo Daszyńskiego to the stadium and from MDM to the Barbakan.
- Podwózka, the app-taxi side job.
- The radio plays real playlists, and new songs drop in with one command.
- Smoother on the iPhone 11, and the first Android build for Google Play.
- The website aroundwarsaw.app goes live.
