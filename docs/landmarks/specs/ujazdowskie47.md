# Aleje Ujazdowskie 47 (site `ujazdowskie47`)

## Identity
Dark grey render among pale neighbours, tall piers on the two lower storeys, arched openings, green pharmacy signs, iron balconies.

## Massing
LoD2's mass kept in its skin. Street front 19.1 m from (-60, 788.5) to (-63.4, 769.7), facing east.

## Facade
Six bays. Arches at street level, a heavy cornice at 9.4 m, three storeys above, attic with balustrade to 24.8 m.

## Roof
LoD2's roof behind the attic.

## Colour and material
Wall #6c6f70, base #5a5d5f, trim #7c7f80, frames #e4e0d6, green #1f7a55; M_STONE.

## Ground and night
Pharmacy glass with plain green bands. Windows light one by one at night. No card: it is a street house.
