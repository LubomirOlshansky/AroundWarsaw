# Kamienica Ławrynowicza (site `lawrynowicz`)

## Identity
Banded yellow and red brick, the two end towers with stepped gables, the middle gable, arcades at street level.

## Massing
LoD2's mass kept in its brick skin. Street front on Aleje Ujazdowskie, 24.9 m, towers 4.9 m wide and 0.6 m proud.

## Facade
Arches and a central gate at street level, four arched storeys above, paired windows in the towers, balconies.

## Roof
LoD2's roof, iron cresting at 21.7 m between the gables.

## Colour and material
Brick #d8b67a, red #a7523a, stone #d9c9a4, glass #2c3944, iron #25272a; M_STONE.

## Ground and night
Dark gate in the centre arch. Windows light one by one at night.

## Detail level and budget
L3, triangle budget 8000. Shots: the front from Aleje Ujazdowskie.
