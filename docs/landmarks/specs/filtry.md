# Stacja Filtrów (site `filtry`)

## Identity
The tall brick water tower with its wider tank, the raised lawns dotted with vents, the red brick chambers along the roads.

## Massing
Tower at (-1951, 1206), shaft radius 7 m, tank radius 7.7 m, about 41 m to the cap, from OSM and the orthophoto.

## Facade
Stone-capped plinth to 1.6 m, three shaft stages with pilasters and string courses, corbel table at 31 m, tank with 16 round-headed windows.

## Roof
A low grey cap with a 5 m mast (F's photo), parapet at 39 m.

## Colour and material
Brick #a84a32, dark brick #8a3a28, stone #c9bda6, cap #5f665f; M_STONE throughout.

## Ground and night
Starynkiewicz's bust on a pale plinth south-east of the tower. Filter beds are flat grass plateaus.

## Detail level and budget
L2, triangle budget 3000. Shots: from the orthophoto and F's aerial photo angles.
