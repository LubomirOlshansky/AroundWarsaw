# Kamienica Wedla (site `wedel`)

## Identity
White ornate fronts, the corner with its bay and pediment, the tall zinc corner roof, the neon zebra with a rider, burgundy awnings.

## Massing
LoD2's mass kept in its skin. Fronts on Górskiego (40 m) and Szpitalna (27 m), corner at (-636.2, 67).

## Facade
Dark banded piers and shop glass to 5.4 m, three white storeys, arched on top, cornice at 19.6 m, 12 and 8 bays.

## Roof
LoD2's mansard in zinc, dormers on the fronts, corner roof between two turrets.

## Colour and material
White #efe9dc, shade #d9d1c0, zinc #71828a, band #6a5f53, awning #6e2a2e; M_STONE, zebra M_LAMP.

## Ground and night
Burgundy awnings over the shops. The zebra glows at night. The sign carries no lettering or logo text.

## Detail level and budget
L3, triangle budget 8000. Shots: the corner, both fronts.
