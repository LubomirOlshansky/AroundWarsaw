# Szpitalna 5 (site `szpitalna5`)

## Identity
Peach plaster with white trim, the sharp corner, iron balconies on Szpitalna, the blind arch high up at the corner.

## Massing
LoD2's mass kept in its plaster skin. Fronts on Szpitalna (31.9 m) and Przeskok (38.7 m), meeting at (-643.4, 92.8).

## Facade
Banded white ground floor with shop glass to 4.9 m, five upper storeys, 10 and 12 bays, pediments on the first floor.

## Roof
LoD2's roof behind the cornice at 24.6 m.

## Colour and material
Plaster #e8c9a6, white #f1ece0, base #e6dfd1, iron #26282a, glass #2e3a44; M_STONE.

## Ground and night
Shop glass at street level. Night windows from the skin on the other faces.

## Detail level and budget
L3 at the fronts, triangle budget 9000. Shots: the corner from both streets.
