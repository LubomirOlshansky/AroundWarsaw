# The Tides (site `tides`)

## Identity
A long bent slab in folded copper panels, irregular windows, a black sloping roof, the north end out over a glass lobby.

## Massing
The OSM outline, 120 m long. Eaves at 19.6 m, roof to 22.8 m, slab from 4.3 m.

## Facade
Dark glazed street level with copper piers, five storeys of wide panes and paired slits placed irregularly.

## Roof
Black, stepping back 3.6 m from the eaves.

## Colour and material
Copper #e09a55, dark #26282b, pane #2f4048, soffit #d7cdbd; M_STONE and M_PANE.

## Ground and night
Glass lobby under the north end. Panes light one by one. No lettering or logo.

## Detail level and budget
L3, triangle budget 8000. Shots: from the road and the north end.
