# Muzeum Narodowe: spec

Site key `muzeum` in `game/scripts/landmarks.gd` (`_find_muzeum`, `_build_muzeum`). Written 2026-11-06 from refs/muzeum_narodowe 1-4 (Commons photos, see SOURCES.md there).

## Massing
The mapped outline straightened (`_simplify`), on a plinth, to the wings' height with a parapet; a raised entrance block at the back of the forecourt (25 m) with a pale green copper roof (`#8fae9f`).

## Facade
Warm sandstone `#cdbfa1`. Tall window strips every 4.4 m (`_bays`) with a stone pier (1 m wide, 0.5 m proud) on every bay boundary of every straight run (`_muzeum_piers`). The entrance block: four square piers, three dark doors in the recessed wall (`_muzeum_doors`), a lintel, three wide steps.

## Forecourt (`_muzeum_forecourt`)
Black spear-topped iron fence across the court's mouth (v -62.7, u -22..24.7) with the gate standing open in the middle, collision except the gate; six flagpoles (11 m) in front of the steps with blue banners of the museum's own design (no sponsor marks).

## Not modelled
Paving of the court (pink-red stone), the pool, benches, the lamps; the army yard hardware is the earlier tank and gun.
