# BGK (Gmach Banku Gospodarstwa Krajowego, Al. Jerozolimskie 7) reference report
Files: refs/bgk/ (8 photos; ortho.jpg 140 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-03; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions +-5 m. Architect Rudolf Swierczynski, built 1928-31, extended later (a south wing and a rebuilt east part; building under renovation 2025). Today also the PGE seat (rooftop PGE sign) and the BGK sign.

## Form and height
- **7 storeys + stepped attic**, ~27 m to the main cornice (27.4 mapped, ok); the corner blocks (east at the Nowy Swiat corner, west) rise two more storeys as **set-back tower-like volumes** (to ~33-35 m), carrying roof signs (BGK red letters, PGE logo).
- Photo 1: the long Jerozolimskie front, a plain stone block with a strong **horizontal cornice above storey 5** (below the top two storeys), flat roof; the east tower block stands forward at right.
- Photo 2 (from Nowy Swiat): the east front stepped back above the cornice; red open-box BGK logo on the roof.
- Photo 4: the west/Bracka end, where the front steps up into a corner block with a low rusticated base.

## Facade
- Material: **grey granite / sandstone ashlar** in large blocks with lighter and darker patches; colour main **#9A9C99** (sunlit light grey) with darker stone panels **#78797A** and shaded parts **#5F6163**; mortar joints visible as a grid (blocks ~0.6 x 0.4 m).
- Window rhythm (photos 5, 6): **paired tall windows**, each pair in a stone-framed bay; piers between pairs are broad (~1.5x window width) and slightly proud; ~3.3 m storey height, window ~1.1 m wide x 2.0 m high, dark brown frames **#4A2E2A**, small-square glazing bars in the upper sash; glass reflecting sky **#8FA3B3**.
- Horizontal bands: a projecting stone string course under the 5th floor and a heavy cornice (photos 1, 2, 6); lower floors have **deeper rustication**, the top two floors smooth with small square attic windows (photo 7).
- Base: ground and first floor in **darker stone #6B6D6E**, tall stepped-vertical windows (grouped) along Jerozolimskie, shop/bank-hall glazing; the ground storey ~5 m.
- Portal / entrance (photo 8): a **gilded monogram 'BGK' in bronze/gold #C8B070** on the pier above a tall central window, flanked by two stone pilasters, small relief panels (stone geometric ornaments) below; iron balconies with black railings on the upper windows (balconettes on storeys 6-7 with grille railings **#2A2F3A**).
- Reliefs (photo 3): at the stepped corner a **frieze of sculpted relief** (Jan Szczepkowski's reliefs, stone figures and tools) in a deep band under the roof edge; photo 3 looks up the east corner showing the flat sculpted frieze under the stepped cornice.
- Top / attic: 2 storeys of smaller square windows; signs on the roof in red/blue (BGK **#C8102E**, PGE blue **#1F4E9C**) and thin antennas.

## Neighbours
