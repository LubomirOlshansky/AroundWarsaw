# Warszawa Gdańska reference report (railway S3e; at the map's north edge)
Files: refs/gdanska/ (3 older photos named by title, 1-3 numbered; ortho.jpg 320 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-01/02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±5 m.

- OSM: Warszawa Gdańska station (-2159, -2775), metro Dworzec Gdański (-2035, -2660). The platforms run E-W at
  about **z -2775..-2745**, x -2240..-2000; the **Trasa / Słomińskiego viaduct** crosses them on the east at
  x ~-1930..-1900 (the wide road on the ortho's right).
- **The ortho (2025-26) shows the station being rebuilt**: an open excavation and new platform works at
  x -2181..-2086, z -2748..-2718, a footbridge with lift towers (1, 3: green steel lift shafts with glass, a long
  footbridge on concrete arches, 3) and a new canopy at the platforms' east end.
- The 1950s station building (2): a low 2-storey flat-roofed block, light grey with a band of windows, the name
  **WARSZAWA GDAŃSKA** in metal letters on the roof edge, a glazed entrance pavilion with a pyramid roof on the
  left. In the ortho it is probably the block at about x -2033..-1982, z -2735..-2683 (check; it may be gone).
- Default for the game: the edge ring as now; a low station block and the platform canopies only if the map box
  reaches them.
