# Pomnik Powstania Warszawskiego (Plac Krasińskich) reference report
Files: refs/krasinskich/ (6 photos; 4 a lamp, 5-6 the square during the 2018 rebuild, kept for the cathedral and
the court; ortho.jpg 260 m square, crop read at 0.107 m/px). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±3 m.

- Position: OSM w1327606552 centre (-1234, -1700). In the ortho: the **main group** stands at the **south-east
  corner of the Supreme Court (Sąd Najwyższy)**, x -1245..-1225, z -1712..-1693, on a raised granite platform
  with broad steps down to the square (stair lines x -1260..-1240, z -1695..-1680); the **small group** (insurgents
  going down into the sewer manhole) stands ~25 m south at about **(-1237, -1672)**, at the west edge of the square.
- The square: grey granite paving x -1240..-1195, z -1700..-1650, open to the street on the south-west
  (Długa / Miodowa side), the Supreme Court's green-glass colonnade to the north-west, Pałac Krasińskich's garden
  and trees to the north-east, the Garrison cathedral's twin towers to the south (6).
- Main group (1, 3): a **big tilted granite slab** (light grey **#B9B6AE**, ~10 m wide, leaning at ~40° like a
  falling wall, ~7 m at its high end) with a second smaller slab; from under it bursts a group of ~12 **bronze
  insurgents** (helmets, rifles, a priest, a girl) running forward, dark bronze **#4B4033**; behind them a broken
  relief wall of rubble and beams. Faces south-west (toward the square / Długa).
- Small group (1 left, 2): ~6 bronze figures round the manhole, on a low dark granite base, life size +.
- Lamps (4): green-grey cast-iron posts with a scrolled crown and a bell lantern.
