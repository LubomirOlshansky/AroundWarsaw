# Filharmonia Narodowa (Jasna 5) reference report
Files: refs/filharmonia/ (3 photos: 1 the corner at dusk with the neon, 2-3 the statues between the columns;
ortho.jpg 140 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±4 m.

- Position: OSM w29090530 centre (-870, -45); ortho: a near-square block **x -906..-844, z -81..-12** (flat grey
  roof with a raised hall roof in the middle), **Jasna on its east side** (x ~-835), Sienkiewicza north,
  Moniuszki south. The main front faces east onto Jasna.
- Front (2, 3): a **giant order of smooth cream-white columns #EDE9DE** (two to three storeys) along the front,
  between them tall windows with iron balconettes, each holding a **white stone statue of a composer**
  (life size +) on the sill; a plain cornice and attic over the colonnade; walls pale cream stone **#E3DED2**.
- The corner (1): rusticated base, the green neon **"fn"** roundel and **SALA KONCERTOWA** in green neon script
  (night). Use as is: it is the institution's name, not a brand.
