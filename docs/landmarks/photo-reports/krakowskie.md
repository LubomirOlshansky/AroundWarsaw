# Krakowskie Przedmieście reference report (K1-K6 leftovers)

Files: refs/krakowskie/ (29 Commons photos 1-29, free licences in SOURCES.md; three GUGiK orthophotos
ortho_n / ortho_m / ortho_s, 420 m square each, 0.137 m/px). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02.
Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south).
Ortho frames (full px): ortho_n x = -837.8 + px*0.1367, z = -1360.0 + py*0.1367; ortho_m x = -769.6 + px*0.1367,
z = -984.0 + py*0.1367; ortho_s x = -674.0 + px*0.1367, z = -630.2 + py*0.1367 (north up).
All ortho numbers are read by eye, about ±3 m. **The orthophoto is blurred (censored) over the block
x -660..-540, z -800..-650 (the Ministry of Culture: Pałac Potockich and its yard)**; values there come from
the photos and OSM only.

Street geometry: the street runs NNW-SSE, about 20-25° east of south going south. Even numbers (Carmelites,
Pałac Prezydencki, Bristol, the University) are on the east side, odd (Europejski, Potocki, Czapski) on the west.
The whole street is pale grey granite (slabs on the walks, setts on the roadway, a darker setts strip on the
roadway's centre north of Mickiewicz at x -670..-655); the roadway is about 7-8 m wide, the walks 8-15 m.

## 1. Pomnik Adama Mickiewicza (photos 1-4)
- Plinth centre **(-626, -1078)** (OSM node -626, -1075). It stands in its own fenced lawn square on the
  **east** side of the roadway: lawn x -652..-604, z -1104..-1050, with rose beds (red, yellow) west of the
  monument (1, 4); trees on the square's east half.
- **Faces west (to the street)**: the main stair flight is on the street side (4: a wide flight of ~8 steps
  with side flights right and left); the ortho also shows steps on the north side.
- Shape (1, 4): a raised granite terrace ~16 m wide x ~10 m deep, ~1.6 m high, with a stone balustrade
  (pale grey-white #D8D4CC, balusters) on its front and sides; four bronze candelabra on posts at the terrace
  corners with **gilt flame tops** (#C9A13A) (2, 3 are close-ups: dark bronze #3A3A32, ornate stems, 2-3 glass
  globes each, ~5 m tall). Centre: a stepped pedestal (light granite #CFCAC0) with a cartouche and bronze
  wreath/foliage group at its base, a tall square shaft (~6 m) with a bronze medallion, then the statue
  (bronze, dark green-grey patina #4A5048, standing, right hand on chest, cloak) ~4.5 m. Total height ~13-14 m.

## 2. Brama Główna Uniwersytetu (photos 5-11) and Pałac Kazimierzowski (16, 17, 9)
- Gate on the east building line at **(-462, -642)** (±4 m), between the green-copper-roofed building to its
  north (ends z ~-648) and the grey-roofed block to its south (starts z ~-636). Faces **WSW** onto the street.
- Width (5, 7, 10): total ~24 m: two white pavilions (~4.5 m wide, ~7 m to the cornice, ~10 m to the top
  of the curved gable with a cartouche, then a lantern + finial up to ~14 m), each with a statue in a niche
  (Minerva / Apollo, stone white), between them the central opening ~6 m with a black wrought-iron gate and a
  crowned eagle cresting; on the outside of each pavilion a pier with an urn and a narrow side gate (iron).
  Colour: white #F2F0EA with pale grey-cream mouldings #DCD6C8; window-like panels; flags hang on them.
- Behind it a straight **tree-lined avenue** (9: limes both sides, cobbles) runs ENE ~230 m to Pałac
  Kazimierzowski (OSM centre (-252, -737)), which closes the vista (5: seen through the gate).
- Pałac Kazimierzowski (16, 17): 3 storeys, ochre-yellow **#E2C77A**, white Corinthian portico of 4 columns
  with a triangular pediment over the centre (2nd-3rd storey), ground floor with round-arched windows and doors
  in a rusticated base, white window surrounds, steep red tile roof **#B5503A** with white chimneys.
  The front faces WSW down the avenue.

## 3. Hotel Bristol (photos 12-15)
- Block x -585..-500, z -960..-890 with a courtyard; street front on the east line of KP from (-585, -955) to
  (-560, -893). **The dome corner is the south-west corner, at KP / Karowa: rotunda centre (-551, -889)**,
  diameter ~7 m (ortho). Karowa runs east along the south side (z ~-890..-880, parked cars).
- Facade (13, 14): cream-ivory **#E9E1C8** (shade #CFC6AC), 5 storeys + a grey zinc mansard **#6F7477**
  with dormers; the corner is rounded with pilasters, a balcony on the 2nd floor and an iron-and-glass canopy
  over the corner entrance (dark iron #2B2B2B, Café Bristol); the corner tower ends in an **open round
  belvedere**: 8 thin columns, a balustrade and a flat dome cap (cream), ~5 m above the roof line. Triangular
  gables over the side risalits. Ground floor shop windows, dark frames.
- Night (15): warm white facade floodlight, the corner canopy lit.

## 4. Pałac Czapskich (ASP) (photos 18, 19)
- Main body OSM centre (-611, -598), red tile roof x -630..-591, z -620..-570; **set back ~90 m west of the
  street and facing east** across a forecourt (x -590..-505) framed by two white-roofed wings (z -660..-630
  and -605..-585).
- Colour (18): **brick-red plaster #D3644E** (shade #B5503F) with white pilasters, quoins, window surrounds
  and cornice **#F2EFE8**; centre risalit with a white triangular pediment holding a sculpted cartouche and a
  small statue, balcony with iron railing over the door; corner pavilions project and are red too; roof red
  tile with a white chimney row and small dormers. 3 storeys.
- The street front (19): two beige 4-storey wings **#E6D6BC** flank the gateway; lamp posts in front.

## 5. Pałac Potockich and the guardhouses (photos 20-23)
- West side opposite Bristol / Wizytki, roughly z -800..-760 on the street line x ~-545 (the ortho is blurred
  here). Street front (20, 21): the **Kordegarda** in the centre, two gate piers with wrought-iron gates on each
  side, then a small 2-storey guardhouse/pavilion each side (left = north, right = south) with **orange-red
  hipped mansard roofs #C5502F** and cream walls **#EDE8DC**.
- The southern guardhouse (21, right): ~12 m wide, 2 storeys + mansard with dormers, 5 bays, plain cream
  plaster with white quoins. The northern one mirrors it.
- Kordegarda (22, 23): one storey, ~22 m wide, ~6 m to the cornice, white **#EFEDE6**, three big round-arched
  windows each side of a central round-arched door (dark wood), shallow pilasters, a balustraded attic with
  **sculpture groups** (stone, grey-white) on the corners and centre.

## 6. Kościół Karmelitów (Wniebowzięcia NMP i św. Józefa) (photos 24, 25)
- Teal copper roof x -588..-539, z -1064..-1023 (ortho_n), **front on the east street line at x ~-590,
  z -1057..-1023, facing west** onto a paved forecourt with parking (x -640..-600, z -1050..-1020), south of
  the Mickiewicz square and north of Pałac Prezydencki.
- Front (24): sandstone **#C9B78C** with ochre stains #B39A62, two storeys. Lower: four engaged columns
  (two pairs flanking the central door, Tuscan) plus pilasters at the ends, three doors (central large,
  two round-arched side doors, dark wood). Upper: a narrower temple front with **four free-standing Corinthian
  columns** on a balustraded balcony, a big arched window between them, two statues in niches, a straight
  entablature and attic with sculpture groups, crowned by a **green copper globe #5E9E8A** with a gilt finial.
  Flanking the upper storey: two **lantern turrets** with round openings and small green caps.

## 7. The granite paving's extent at Nowy Świat's top (ortho_s)
- The pale granite surface of KP continues over the Copernicus square: **Kopernik on a round red-brown setts
  disc ~15 m across at (-410, -462)** (OSM statue (-411, -460)), the square's paving x -450..-390,
  z -500..-445. A crossing (white bars) at (-440, -486).
- South of z ~-445 (Nowy Świat between the Staszic palace and the west row) the roadway is ~7 m and lighter
  grey, likely setts, down to Świętokrzyska (z ~-355), where asphalt with red bike lanes begins. Photo check of
  this stretch still open (no ground photo in this set).

## 8. Street dressing (photos 26-29)
- Café gardens (26, 27): low black or wooden barriers, white and dark-red market umbrellas, wooden decks,
  planters; white awnings over shopfronts on the west row near Bednarska; granite bollards (cylinders ~0.8 m,
  #BDB8AE) every ~4 m along the walk edge; young street trees (limes) in the walk on both sides.
- Lamps: black cast-iron historic lamp posts (~5 m, one or two lanterns, scrolled arms) every ~25 m on both
  walks (19-21, 28).
- Hotel Europejski (28, 29): white-cream **#EEEAE0** neo-renaissance block x -680..-590, z -930..-840 with
  a curved green-grey glass roof edge (ortho: glazed rim), ground-floor round arches, the street corner rounded.
- No carriage (dorożka) rank seen in these photos or the ortho; leave it out or ask F.
