# Muzeum Powstania Warszawskiego (Elektrownia Tramwajów Miejskich) reference report
Files: refs/mpw/ (5 photos: 1, 5, 6 street fronts, 4 the observation tower top, 7 aerial from the WTT; ortho.jpg
260 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±3 m.

## Layout (ortho; ul. Grzybowska along the north edge at z ~95-110, Przyokopowa on the west at x ~-2970,
Towarowa on the east at x ~-2860)
- **Brick front block with a red tile hipped roof**: x -2955..-2932, z 121..138, set back from Grzybowska behind a
  paved forecourt and the entrance gate; 3 storeys, plus a tall gabled brick end (6, the stepped-gable corner).
- **The long power-station hall** along Przyokopowa: x -2955..-2937, z 160..230+, grey-blue metal roof with a
  roof light; its street front (1, 5, 6): red brick **#9E4A36** with buff brick bands and a cream stone cornice
  **#E4DBC6**, two storeys of tall **round-arched windows** (about 14 bays), a 1.5 m brick wall with iron railings
  in front; at the north end a **3-storey brick tower block (~18 m) with a cream gable top** (6) and a white-plastered
  wing (1, 5) hung with long white-red banners.
- **The main halls**: x -2938..-2898, z 165..203, grey-blue pitched roofs with round vents, a glazed roof light
  at (-2905, 195).
- **Observation tower** (4, 7): a square grey concrete shaft **#5D5F60** about 4 x 5 m at about **(-2946, 156)** in
  the yard south of the front block, ~32 m tall, with the **Kotwica sign in white** near the top and a cantilevered
  steel-mesh viewing cage on top (black, a flag pole).
- **Park Wolności and the Memorial Wall**: the wall (the names of the fallen) runs from about (-2905, 140) south-
  south-east to (-2875, 228), ~95 m long, dark rusted steel / bronze plates **#4B3A30**, ~3 m high; a gravel path
  along its east side and the park (lawn, young trees, benches) between it and Towarowa. The Liberty Bell
  ("Monter") stands in the park (not located in the ortho).
- Yard: grey concrete paving between the blocks, a few trees; a small pavilion at (-2955, 145).
