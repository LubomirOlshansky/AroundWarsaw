# Most Poniatowskiego reference report (towers, deck, spans, viaduct)

## Positions (ortho, checked against the map's `towers`)
- The four tower groups stand where the river spans meet the embankments; **the map's tower centres match the
  orthophoto within ~2 m**: west bank north (927, -150) and south (936, -124) over Wybrzeże Kościuszkowskie /
  Wisłostrada; east bank north (1415, -315) and south (1426, -285) over Wał Miedzeszyński. Deck axis bearing:
  about 18.6° north of east going east (dx 489, dz -165, ~516 m between the tower pairs).
- Each group (ortho_towers_*): a crenellated **arcaded pavilion ~15 m long x ~5 m** along the deck's outer edge,
  the **square tower shaft (~7 x 7 m)** at one end, and on the side away from the deck a **stair down to the lower
  road**: on the south groups a straight flight ~5 x 10 m ending in a **round stair turret ~5 m across**
  (west-south group's turret at about (936, -114)); on the north groups a curved ramp/stair. The roads below run
  N-S under the deck between the pairs' bases.
- The south-west group's shaft is at its **land (west) end** with the arcade running toward the river (photo 5:
  the Muzeum Narodowe behind on the right). Default for the others: mirror it (shaft at the land end); check 1 and 11.

## Heights (estimated from photos 5, 14, 25 against people and the deck; ±3 m)
- Pavilion: crest of the merlons **~7.5 m above the deck walk**, arcade openings ~3.5 m high.
- Tower shaft: cornice of the shaft **~16 m** above the deck; the belfry storey with three narrow arched windows per
  face and its merlon crown to **~19 m**, the corner ball finials to **~20 m above the deck**.
- Below the deck the tower continues down to the lower road: the map has base -13.9 vs deck -4.3, i.e. **~9.6 m**,
  so ~30 m from the lower road to the finials (photo 25 agrees).

## The towers' faces (photos 3, 5, 14, 24, 25, 19, 4)
- Colour: warm cream render / sandstone **#E8DFC8** (shade #CFC4A8), the lower storeys below the deck grey-cream
  stone blocks **#B9B2A2**; 19 shows a cooler grey-white after renovation **#DCDAD2**.
- Shaft: plain walls with a few small round-arched windows and one oculus, a cornice, then a narrower belfry storey
  with **three tall arched slits per face**, a **crenellated crown of stepped merlons with scrolls** and ball finials
  at the corners; a black iron bracket lantern on the shaft's deck-side face (14, 25).
- Pavilion (24, 5): a 4-5 bay **arcade of round arches on short columns** (open on the deck side; the river-side
  bays glazed with leaded diamond panes in arched windows, 24), triangular stone insets over the arches, a
  string course, then the **scrolled merlon parapet** with balls; a smaller 2-3 bay arcade beyond the shaft (5).
- Under the deck (17, 19, 4): the tower bases have big round-arched portals, triple-arched windows, a red-brick
  infill panel framed in white stone on the Powiśle face (4), stone balconies.

## Deck, balustrades, lamps (9, 10, 11, 13, 16, 21, 26)
- Railing: **black cast-iron balustrade ~1.2 m** with ornamental panels (scrolls, roundels) on a **red-painted
  fascia girder #B8484E** (faded pink-red, rust streaks) along both deck edges (9, 11, 22).
- Over each river pier, a **stone pedestal block** projects from the railing with a carved coat-of-arms cartouche
  (the governorates' arms, e.g. Kalisz, 9) and a small turret top; grey granite **#9C9A94**.
- Lamps/poles: tall steel **tram catenary masts** every ~30 m on both kerbs carrying the wires and street lamps;
  historic black lamp posts on the walks near the towers (14, 25). Deck: 2+2 traffic lanes, a central tram track
  pair, walks ~4 m each side (10, 26).

## River spans and piers (2, 11, 13, 21, 22, 27, 29-31; ortho)
- Steel **open-web braced arches under the deck** (two-hinged arch ribs with diagonal bracing, painted grey
  **#7C8288**, 11, 22) springing from **masonry piers** of grey granite blocks with rounded cutwater ends (29: a
  pier ~9 m wide, ~12 m above the water to the deck).
- Pier centres on the deck axis read from the ortho (bulges on the deck's edges; ±6 m): (1036, -171),
  (1106, -195), (1178, -219); the rest stand on the dry east floodplain under trees (estimate every ~72 m in x:
  ~(1250, -243), ~(1322, -267)). The west span from the towers to the first pier looks longer (~110 m) and may
  hide a pier near the west bank in the deck's shadow: check against photo 22.
- The deck girders are visible in side view as a straight steel edge above the arches (red fascia on top).

## The viaduct over Powiśle (17, 19, 20, 32; ortho_west)
- West of the towers the deck continues as a **reinforced-concrete arch viaduct**: wide shallow arches with thick
  ribbed spandrels, light grey concrete **#B8B6B0** (19: big arch over Wybrzeże; 17: under the deck, arched
  cross-walls with a door and windows in the pier buildings), Al. 3 Maja and Solec passing beneath.
- A memorial plaque on the viaduct wall at Al. 3 Maja (28).
