# Pomnik Stefana Starzyńskiego (Plac Bankowy) reference report
Files: refs/starzynski/ (4 photos, ortho.jpg 120 m square centred on OSM). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±3 m.

- Position: OSM w345079729 **(-1499, -1101)**, on the plaza at the foot of the Błękitny Wieżowiec (the glass tower
  behind it in every photo), north-west corner of Plac Bankowy, in a small lawn and shrub bed.
- Form: a **seated mayor sinking into a huge faceted, tilted bronze "armchair"/cloak** (folded plates like
  broken slabs), hands on its edge, head and shoulders only above it; bronze **#3D3A33**, ~3 m tall, ~4 m wide.
  It stands on a **low rough grey granite block ~4 x 2.5 x 1.2 m** with "STARZYŃSKI" cut in large letters (2).
- Faces south-east, out to the square (the tower's glass curtain wall is behind).
