## Form and height
- A plain **rectangular tower** (about 28 x 32 m), **flat roof**, with a lower stepped side: on the west the face steps in two blocks (photo 1: at ~1/3 height the left edge is set back 3 m, the tower stands on an 8-storey base block on the left).
- The roof edge has a thin 1.2 m **white parapet and a dark band with small plant windows** (photo 1, top two storeys are dark panels with a light louvre strip).
- Roof (ortho): an L-shaped glass/steel plant enclosure, green sedum strip on the low roof, an L terrace with decking and trees on the 8th-storey roof of the wing.

## Facade
- **White-framed grid**: a continuous frame of **white/cream stone-coloured vertical piers #E3E1DA** every ~3.0 m with horizontal white bands each storey (photo 1: each frame 3.0 m wide x 3.9 m high), blue glass **#6B94B8** in sun with a dark-blue lower edge, black spandrel panels **#1A1D22** in a stepped pattern: every ~4th storey has a black band on one or two bays (photo 1: black patches at ~storey 8, 15, 22, 32 and the top two storeys), so the face looks "pixelated" with irregular dark patches.
- Lower storeys: the ground two storeys have taller dark glass and white frame, cafes (photo 1 left: "Caffe Nero" under the wing).
- The adjacent lower wing (Generation Park Z): the same white grid, black side faces on the east, 12 storeys, a roof sign strip with tenant logos (F5, dcg, VISTRA, CRIDO; photo 1).

## Base and neighbours
- Base: a 2-storey glass podium with white columns; frontage on Towarowa (the road in the lower right of the ortho); trams on Towarowa and the Rondo tram loop.
- Neighbours: Warsaw Unit (NE, across the Rondo), the Skyliner/Hub (NW); south: Towarowa 22 building site (photo 6), railway to Warszawa Zachodnia.
