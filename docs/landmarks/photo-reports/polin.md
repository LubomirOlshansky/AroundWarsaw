# POLIN Museum + Ghetto Heroes Monument: reference report

Game coordinates (x east, z south, metres; -Z is north). Sources: 25 Commons photos (SOURCES.md) and `ortho.png`
(GUGiK, 10 px per metre, left x=-2210, right x=-1990, top z=-1790, bottom z=-1600; game x = -2210 + px/10,
z = -1790 + py/10). Confidence: ortho numbers ±1 m; photo-scale numbers ±15 %. No photo gives a measured
height; heights come from people in the photos and from shadow lengths in the ortho (they agree).

"Photo N" means file `cNN_*` for the museum; in section (f) it means `dNN_*` (monument). Some numbers are gaps because dupes were dropped.

## 0. The building in plan (from the ortho)

A rotated box, 68 x 68 m (not 94 x 89: that is the bounding box of the turned square). Corners:
T (north) = (-2091.7, -1745.4), R (east) = (-2057.0, -1686.6), B (south) = (-2117.6, -1653.6), L (west) = (-2150.6, -1711.9).
Sides: **east front T-R** (faces ENE, outward normal (0.861, -0.508), faces the plaza and monument), **south front R-B**
(faces SSE, to Anielewicza street and the parking), **west front B-L** (faces WSW, to the big lawn), **north front L-T**
(faces NNW, to the rear car park). The front line direction is 59.4 deg from +x toward +z.
Height: **about 18 m** (people at the canyon in photos 13 and 15 give 17.5-18 m; the building's shadow in the ortho,
~11 m long, with the monument's 11 m wall casting 6.8 m, gives ~17.6 m). The estimate "box 23 m tall" looks 5 m too high.

## (a) Facade

- Skin: vertical glass fins, each a laminated glass sheet **~0.27-0.30 m wide (about 3.5 per metre), ~1.3-1.4 m tall**,
  stacked in rows with a horizontal joint every ~1.35 m, rows offset so joints do not line up (photo 21, 26).
  Fins stand off the wall at a slant like louvres/shingles: **depth ~0.10-0.15 m**, giving a dark vertical shadow line between
  fins, a sawtooth edge at the bottom and a dark ~0.4 m recess above a thin white gravel strip and a pale concrete kerb at ground.
- Glass colour: grey-green to grey-blue, not pale green. By daylight: sunlit #adb3ad (photo 23), mid #889a9f (photo 20),
  shade #566466 (photo 16); under a blue sky it reflects to blue #3d5d82 (photos 6). Overall a cool silver-grey that
  goes blue with sky. Current #9fb0a8 sits inside this range but is slightly too green: use ~#8a9ba0 for the lit mid tone.
- Behind the fins: dark blue-grey, reads as shadow (~#3c4a4c). Copper: I see **no copper-orange tone anywhere** in 25 photos;
  the "pre-patinated copper mesh" the architects mention reads as the pale grey-green behind the glass. Do not add copper colour.
- Lettering: black-printed (frit) overlapping Latin and Hebrew letters (P O L I N, פולין) on every fin, letters ~0.25-0.3 m
  high, random orientation and overlap (photos 8, 21). **It never reads as a word.** From a distance it only gives the glass a
  speckled, mottled texture (darker at edges). So: a noise/speckle texture, no readable "POLIN".
- A ~1.2 m wide teal-blue glass band crowns the perimeter above the roof edge (ortho), with a railing on top.
- East front also has two long dark horizontal band windows at ~4-5 m (about 10 m long) and one small lit slot south of the canyon (photo 20).

## (b) Canyon entrance, east front

- Where: along the east front, **40-56 m from the north corner** (12-28 m from the south corner), centre at
  (-2067.3, -1704.1); i.e. in the southern third. Axis runs WSW into the building (direction (-0.86, 0.51)).
- Shape: a tall wedge, narrow at the top, wide at the bottom. Base opening **~15 m** along the front (ortho: 15.4 m),
  half height **~8 m**, top **~3.5 m**; it reaches almost the full height (~17 m of 18 m) (photos 13, 15, 20).
- Walls: the **north wall is curved** (concave, sweeping up and in, flaring outward at its base into the plaza); the **south side
  is a straight plane leaning back** with a dark inclined glazed edge. Wall colour is cast concrete in warm sand: sunlit ~#b8a487,
  typical ~#a39378, shade ~#6f6354 (photos 15, 16). **#d8c8a6 is too light and too yellow.**
- Floor: the pale travertine-like patch in front (ortho): corners about (-2070.0, -1711.5), (-2067.5, -1711.8),
  (-2059.5, -1699.0), (-2062.5, -1697.8): 15 m long, 3-5 m wide, lighter than the grey setts.
- Glazing: deep set, bronze-dark curtain wall (~1.2 m mullions) with a revolving/double door cluster, looks near-black by day
  and warm yellow at dusk (photo 12). No steps: the plaza paving runs flush into the canyon floor (the queue stands on it).

## (c) West and north fronts

- **The same cut comes out on the west front**: the glazed "lens" window (photos 0, 19). It is a wide shallow lens
  ~35-40 m wide (about half the front), ~10 m tall, bottom near ground, top ~3-4 m below the roofline, framed by a thin dark curved
  lip, clear glass with ~1.5 m mullions, warm-lit at night. Its centre is about (-2132, -1680) on the west front.
  The roof slot (d) is its skylight and the canyon is its other end: one curved cut runs from the east canyon through the whole box.
- A concrete plinth/retaining wall ~1.5 m high (board-marked concrete, #a9a7a0) runs along the west and north fronts, with a
  1.5-3 m deep walkway or light-well beside it (ortho: pale strip, railing), so fins start above ground there (photos 1, 2).
- North front: plain fins only, no opening seen. Roof plant (d) sits behind it.
- South front: plain fins to the ground, setts to the base, a hung banner board in photo 6. At the SE corner a sunken stair
  goes down at x -2068 to -2060, z -1683 to -1677 (service/lower entrance; ortho).

## (d) Roof (flat, pale grey membrane ~#bcbcb6, parapet and teal glass-edge band)

- The slot: a gently S-curved glazed cut, ~48 m long, about (-2074, -1700) at the east end (10 m behind the canyon), through
  (-2089, -1698.7), (-2100, -1692), (-2111, -1684.4) to (-2122, -1674.5), roughly 2-2.5 m wide, 7-8 m inside the west front.
  Its south side is a **higher curved lip with a ribbed (standing-seam) grey surface** sweeping up and over; the slot's shadow falls onto the
  north side. Shadow of ~2.2 m under a 58 degree sun gives a lip about 3-3.5 m above the north roof (LoD2 says 2.3 m: same order).
- The "3 m hump" near (-2127, -1702): **nothing visible in the ortho**: flat membrane with faint radial staining around about
  (-2126, -1704), so a barely domed drainage fall, and no shadow. The 3 m is probably a LoD2 artefact. I would not model a hump
  (at most a <0.3 m dome).
- Other roof things: plant room ~(-2114 to -2096, -1733 to -1724) with six round fans; a ~8 m lift/plant box at
  about (-2100 to -2092, -1706 to -1698) with grilles; two blue three-panel skylight groups near (-2114 to -2109, -1717 to -1713)
  and (-2106 to -2101, -1712 to -1707), each on a small dark upstand; roof joints every ~10 m running NNW-SSE.

## (e) Ground

- East plaza: small light-grey granite setts (#a9a9a6), laid in rows, with a thin dark line (inlay/drain slot) running NNW-SSE
  parallel to the front **~10 m east of it** (from about (-2093, -1752) to (-2062, -1700) and on). The plaza stretches to the monument
  and to Zamenhofa/Karmelicka edges; a few trees in the south and east with dark mulch beds.
- Lawns: large lawn west and south-west (the west front), grass strips and benches south of the parking; rear (north) a car park
  with lawn island. Parking SE with blue disabled bays near (-2060, -1680 region, ortho px 1480,1110).
- The pale travertine patch and the sunken stair are in (b)/(c). Photo 17 shows bollards (yellow) at the plaza edge of the south corner.

## (f) Monument (Pomnik Bohaterów Getta, Rapoport/Suzin, 1948)

- **Exact spot (ortho):** platform centre **(-2031, -1741)**, wall centre ~(-2030.4, -1741): about 10 m east and 6 m north of the
  estimate (-2041, -1735). The platform is a rotated rectangle, **16 x 30 m**, corners (-2031.4,-1758.3), (-2045.1,-1750.1),
  (-2029.8,-1724.1), (-2017.7,-1731.6)... its long axis runs 27 deg from south toward east (direction (0.457, 0.889)). 
  Edge: 1.2 m darker border, lighter inner slabs.
- **Facing:** the wall's long axis follows the platform's long axis, so its two faces point **WSW (-0.89, +0.46) toward the museum**
  (fighters relief, steps) and **ENE (+0.89, -0.46) toward Zamenhofa** (deportation relief). Fighters face the museum.
- **Wall size:** ~**9.5 m wide** (photos 0, 11 give wall width/height 0.87, so 9.6 m at 11 m high; ortho top 8.8 m), **~11 m high**,
  **~3 m deep** (2.9-3.6 m). Slightly tapered: bottom a little wider than top, faces lean in a few degrees (photo 1). Not 11 m wide.
- **Skin:** dark grey-green labradorite slabs ~1 m square with thin light joints: lit #85868b, shade #4d5154. Light grey granite
  frame around each relief (#a8a8a2). Black polished plinth/steps #25272a with a blue-green sparkle (photo 6).
- **Steps/platform:** on the west face **4-5 low risers** (~0.15-0.2 m each, ~0.8 m total) across about 12-17 m, black granite, topped by a ~0.5 m black plinth under
  the wall (a bit more than "three steps"). Wreath/flower bed along the lowest step.
- **Fighters relief (west face):** a tall recess in the centre, ~3 m wide x ~7 m high, bottom ~1.2 m above the wall base,
  dark bronze figures (#3a3a36, greenish patina) standing out of the stone; a small plaque row at the base (photos d07, d12).
- **Deportation relief (east face, "Pochód na zagładę"):** a wide horizontal panel ~6 m wide x ~3.6 m high, bottom ~2.8 m
  above the base, centred, stone-coloured bas-relief with a rough frame (photos 1, 2, 8).
- **Menorahs:** two bronze seven-branch menorahs each held by lions on a ~1.5 m square, ~1 m high polished granite block,
  standing at the two ends of the west steps ~12.5 m apart, at about (-2040.2, -1743) and (-2033.6, -1732.4), ~7 m in front of the wall
  centre (photo 0, 6, 11); a third, with a flame, stands in front of the relief at the wall foot (photo d07).

## Contradictions with the current estimates

1. **Box 23 m tall**: photos and shadows say ~18 m.
2. **Glass #9fb0a8 pale green fins**: real glass is greyer and bluer (#8a9ba0 mid, #adb3ad lit, #566466 shade, blue under sky); slightly too green. No copper tone is visible.
3. **Canyon walls #d8c8a6**: too light and yellow: ~#a39378 typical (#b8a487 lit, #6f6354 shade), warm concrete.
4. **Monument 11 x 3.6 x 11 m**: width ~9.5 m (11 m only if you take the platform), height 11 m, depth ~3 m; stone is dark grey-green slabs; **steps are 4-5 low risers**, not three. Position is ~10 m east and ~6 m north of (-2041,-1735).
5. Museum outline: it is a 68 m square turned 30 deg, not an axis-aligned 94 x 89 box; a box in game-coordinates should be rotated (east front normal (0.861,-0.508)).
6. The roof "3 m hump" near (-2127,-1702) is not visible in the ortho (flat, faint dome stains).
7. The canyon, roof slot and west lens are one cut; do not model them as three separate things.
