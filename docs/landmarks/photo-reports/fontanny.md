# Multimedialny Park Fontann (Podzamcze) reference report
Files: refs/fontanny/ (6 photos: 1 the fountain ring by day, 4 the view from the escarpment; ortho.jpg 260 m). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±6 m (centres from OSM/Nominatim).
- Position: centre about **(-868, -2208)**, in the strip between the Old Town escarpment's park (west) and
  Wisłostrada (east, the 6-lane road on the ortho's right).
- Ortho: a light paved plaza shaped like a **long S / treble-clef**: a big round fountain basin (~30 m, dark ring
  jets) at the north end and a smaller circular one at the south, joined by a curving channel, framed by concentric
  arc lines in the paving; a long straight **water channel** along the road side; lawns with paths round it; a
  curved low pavilion at the south end.
- Photos: a shallow granite basin with dozens of vertical jets (to ~8 m, a central one higher), a low white stone
  rim people sit on; evenings: the light-and-laser show (sound, coloured jets, a water screen).
