# Plac Piłsudskiego reference report
Files: refs/pilsudskiego/ (6 photos; 2 is a 1930s night photo, kept for the flag poles only; ortho.jpg 320 m
square, frame x = -944.8 + px*0.1042, z = -956.1 + py*0.1042). Also refs/grob/ (the Tomb).
Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02. ±3 m.
**The ortho is blurred (censored) east of x ~-720 between z -870 and -640** (the ministry block by Hotel Europejski).

## The square
- Paved area **x -840..-718, z -868..-735**, a slight trapezoid; grey granite slabs **#B4B4B0** in a grid of
  ~6 m squares outlined by darker grey bands **#8E8F8C** (1, 3: a chequer of lighter and darker panels).
- Lawns: north strip x -820..-760, z -878..-860 (by Królewska); south lawn x -800..-720, z -733..-685, with a
  red-brown strip of paving along its north edge (x -790..-740, z ~-738).
- Edges: Królewska and the Metropolitan's ring road on the north (Metropolitan x -830..-760, z -950..-880);
  Ossolińskich/the east road (x -735..-705) on the east, Hotel Europejski beyond; the south road toward Wierzbowa.
- Tall lamp/flag masts: about 10 thin poles round the paved field (1).

## West side: the Saxon Palace site (ortho, 2025-26)
- **West of x ~-830 the ortho shows the reconstruction site of the Saxon Palace**: open archaeological
  excavations (cellar walls, brown earth) x -905..-800, z -905..-850 and z -760..-650, two **white temporary halls**
  (x -880..-830, z -815..-790 and x -870..-815, z -760..-735), a red crane. The game's grass here is the older
  state; decide with F whether to show a building site (default: leave the grass, it is the Saski Garden edge).
- The **Grób Nieznanego Żołnierza** (the three arches of the old colonnade, sentries) stands between the two halls
  at about **(-871, -773)** (OSM w72833763 centre (-867, -769)); see refs/grob.

## Monuments on the square
- **Smolensk memorial (2010 victims)** (5, 6): a black stone block shaped as a **steep stair seen from the side**
  (stepped on one side, sheer on the other), ~10 m tall, ~4 m deep, ~6 m long, on a low glass-and-water base;
  at **(-768, -738)**, the square's south-east part, steps facing east. Colour polished black **#1E1E22**.
- A standing bronze statue (Lech Kaczyński monument) at about **(-745, -823)** on the east half (its long shadow
  in the ortho); low dark granite plinth.
- Piłsudski's statue (4): OSM (-674, -818), across the east road by the ministry (in the blurred part): bronze
  standing marshal in a greatcoat, both hands on the sabre, on a **rough grey granite block ~2.5 x 2.5 x 3 m**
  with "JÓZEF PIŁSUDSKI / I MARSZAŁEK POLSKI" and two bronze eagle badges on its corners. Total ~6.5 m.
