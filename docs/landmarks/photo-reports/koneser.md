# Centrum Praskie Koneser (Ząbkowska 27/31) reference report
Files: refs/koneser/ (6 photos: 1 and 4 the gatehouse on Ząbkowska, 2 the gate at night, 5 the street front,
6 the inner plaza; ortho.jpg 260 m centred at (1426, -2355)). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±6 m (centres from OSM/Nominatim).
- The former vodka works fills the block south of **Ząbkowska** (the street along the ortho's south part): low
  red-brick halls round a **paved central plaza** with new glass blocks, a green zig-zag roof (ortho centre-right).
- **Gatehouse (1, 4)**: a small **neo-Gothic red-brick lodge #B05A3E** with a steep grey slate roof, a spiky
  corner turret with a slate cone, a pointed-arch door, white stone details; next to it the **iron gate arch** with
  the lettered sign (parody the real name on the gate) between brick piers (2, 4).
- Halls (5, 6): 2-3 storey red brick with segment-arched windows, stepped gables; the plaza in grey concrete slabs
  with a hedge strip; a **tall brick chimney** in the yard (6); a glass bridge with diagonal steel bracing (6).
