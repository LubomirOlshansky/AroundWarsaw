# Pomnik Bohaterów Warszawy (Warszawska Nike) reference report
Files: refs/nike/ (4 photos, ortho.jpg 120 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±3 m.

- Position: OSM w1559054664 **(-1010, -1317)**, on the lawn of the green between Trasa W-Z (Aleja Solidarności,
  north) and Podwale/the Old Town's edge, inside a horseshoe-shaped footpath loop (ortho: path loop round the
  plinth open to the south-east; the monument casts its long shadow north-east).
- Form (1-4): a tall, slightly tapering **square granite pylon ~3 x 3 m at the base, ~9 m high**, warm light grey
  granite **#C8C0B0**; inscription "BOHATEROM WARSZAWY 1939-1945" in dark letters low on the face toward the path
  (the side seen with Pałac Krasińskich / the Old Town behind). On top a **dark bronze Nike #2E302C** (about 5 m),
  lunging forward, left arm up, the right swinging a sword up and back, cloak flying: overall ~14 m.
- Setting: low shrub beds and young trees round the plinth, lawn; the Old Town's edge houses visible behind in 1 and 4.
- Open question: which way the figure faces (photos don't show the compass): default **north-east, toward Trasa
  W-Z / the Old Town**, as the shadow and photo 4 suggest.
