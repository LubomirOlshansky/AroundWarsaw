# Bazar Różyckiego (Targowa / Ząbkowska) reference report
Files: refs/bazar_rozyckiego/ (1 photo 2023; ortho.jpg 160 m). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±6 m (centres from OSM/Nominatim).
- Position: centre **(1097, -2024)**; ortho: the market yard between Targowa (west) and Brzeska (east), about
  60 x 70 m: on the north-west a dense mass of **old low stalls** with patched roofs in grey, tarpaulin blue and
  rust; on the south-east a grid of **~15 new green-roofed kiosks** (2023 rebuild) in rows with walkways; a red
  play/sport area on the north-east.
- Photo: the old stalls (tin and tarp roofs, awnings, crates) facing the new **dark green box pavilions #2F6B55**
  with flat white-edged roofs, separated by a narrow paved lane; tenements all round. No gate photo: open.
