Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-03; licences in SOURCES.md. Positions +-5 m; roofs lean NE by ~15-20 m in the ortho.

## Position (centrum.json)
- No landmark entry; rows: **tower A: x -2705..-2651, z 545..602, h 130**, **tower B: x -2706..-2643, z 612..648, h 130** and the **86 m round-ended tower** (the Crowne Plaza hotel; OSM kind 'hotel', **x -2723..-2689, z 487..538, h 85**). - **Height check**: photo 5 shows tower B visibly lower than tower A (about 10-15 %); OSM gives both 130 m. Treat B as 108-115 m unless a better source says otherwise.

## Form and height
- Towers A and B: **chamfered irregular pentagon plans** ~50 x 55 m, flat roofs with a 1.5 m parapet and a light silver edge, tapered/stepped faces toward Rondo (the NE sides are angled ~35 deg, ortho). They stand SW-NE, **one behind the other along Jana Pawła/Towarowa**, joined at the ground by a 3-storey **podium** between them. The cut corners on the long street faces give a zig-zag in the ortho.
- Tower A (photo 5, left; photo 1): blue glass, crown stepped on one side (cranes in 2019 photos), roof plant box in the centre.
- The **86 m round-ended tower (Crowne Plaza)** at the NW (photos 2, 3): a stadium-shaped plan (half-round on the Rondo side, flat cut on the rail side), 20 storeys, a roof ring of a glass rail.
- Crown Plaza ground: curved dark-grey band (3 m high, "CROWNE PLAZA" letters) over a double-height curved glass hall, a canopy at the corner (photo 2, 3).

## Facade
- Towers A/B: blue-green reflective glass **#6A93B3**, shade **#2D4C66**; fine dark mullions every 1.5 m, storey ~4 m (130 m / 32 storeys); the NE faces are darker **#243845**; ground floors double-height transparent glass with steel columns.
- Crowne Plaza (photo 2): **cream/white metal-clad fins #E8E4DA** with narrow vertical windows (one window per 1.5 m bay, 4 narrow slots, storey 3.4 m), deep vertical fins, a dark charcoal base band **#3A3C40** at the third floor.
- Podium (ortho): a dark glass-and-steel roof/atrium, a rectangular **glazed skylight about 8 x 12 m at x -2678..-2668, z 598..612** and green plant strips beside it; between the towers there is a garden court.

## Roof
- Tower A roof: a large grey plant block (~15 x 20 m) in the middle, fan units along the edges, rooftop in light grey **#B8BCBF**; tower B roof: several grey plant boxes along its southeast side and an F-shaped roof enclosure.
- Crowne Plaza roof: light plant boxes in the middle, the half-round end has a 3-bay glazed ring/louvre.

## Night
No night photos. The towers have regular office lighting; the Crowne Plaza sign letters are lit white.

## Neighbours
- North-west: Skyliner (see skyliner.md); south: Towarowa and a plot with a round-about; east: the tram and rail corridor of Towarowa/Jana Pawła II; west: Wola housing blocks on Prosta; a 4-storey hotel office block at the southern end (ortho).
