# Mennica Legacy Tower (Prosta 20) + west building reference report
Files: refs/mennica/ (12 photos: 1-3 from the earlier set, 4-12 gathered 2026-11-03; ortho.jpg earlier frame, ortho_wide.jpg 200 m square in ortho.txt, north up). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Licences in SOURCES.md. Positions +-5 m.

## Position (centrum.json)
- Tower outline 'Mennica Legacy Tower' (22 points): **x -2134..-2089, z 65..157**. Row h 140 (mh 13) at **x -2134..-2089, z 92..157** is the shaft (13 -> 140 m); a podium/ground ring to 13 m over the same box; a **20 m low wing at x -2130..-2100, z 65..105** on the north (the fountain side). Real: 140 m, 33 storeys, completed 2020.
- West building 'Budynek Zachodni' (32 points): **x -2178..-2137, z 76..173**: 28 m main (mh 8) and a 36 m taller part (mh 28) at x -2178..-2137, z 103..173. Real: ~8-10 storeys, curved glass front onto the courtyard.
- Between the two a courtyard/plaza (x -2137..-2134 z 100..160) with the fountain, granite benches and the steel ring sculpture (photo 9); a grid of 18 small posts (OSM rows h 13, 1 m wide) is the row of **steel columns** at the ground floor (photos 9, 10).

## Form and height
- Shaft: **elliptical/rounded plan with curved glass faces**, wider in the middle of its height (a slight bulge, photo 4, 6, 7), flat top with a thin parapet; no crown, no mast, no setbacks. The north-west edge is flatter, the south-east faces curve (photo 8).
- Ground floor: **double-height open arcade on tall round stainless-steel columns** (~0.9 m diameter, ~8 m high, spacing ~8 m, photos 9, 10) with a recessed copper/terracotta-coloured glass lobby behind (**#8A4A32**).
- Top 3-4 storeys: the same facade, a slightly lighter top band (technical floor, photo 4).

## Facade
- Reflective **silver-blue curved glass #86A6C4** in sun, **#35516B** in shade (photos 4, 12), with **vertical silver fins** every ~1.5 m (a distinct vertical rhythm; storey ~4.2 m, 33 storeys, 140 m) and storey spandrels slightly darker; the cladding is continuous with no big bands (photo 4).
- Photos 5-7 show the building under construction: concrete floor plates, the upper ~10 storeys unglazed, core ahead of the curtain wall (useful for the floor-plate and core layout).
- West building (photos 4, 7, 10): glass, curved front, lighter **#9CB7CF**, copper-coloured soffits at the ground floor.

## Roof and ground
- Roof (ortho_wide): flat, a smaller plant core, round edges; the rooftop lit sign "Mennica" is not visible in these photos.
- Plaza (photos 9, 10): grey granite paving, a round fountain basin, curved granite benches, a large steel ring (~4 m) standing on the plaza next to the tower, trees along Prosta; the old Norblin factory buildings and Browary/Norblin development behind to the west (photo 11).

## Neighbours
- West: Norblin factory (red brick, see norblin.md) with a glass annex; north: Prosta/Rondo ONZ (the tower stands at the south-west corner of Rondo ONZ, photo 8: view from Prosta toward the Rondo); south-east: 1960s residential slabs (photos 5, 6 right edge).
