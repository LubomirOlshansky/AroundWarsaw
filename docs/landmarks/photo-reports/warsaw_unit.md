# Warsaw Unit (Rondo Daszyńskiego 1) reference report
Files: refs/warsaw_unit/ (6 photos; ortho.jpg 160 m square, ortho_wide.jpg 520 m square, frames in ortho.txt, north up). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-03; licences in SOURCES.md. Positions +-5 m. The roof leans NE in the ortho (tower perspective): read the footprint from OSM.

## Position (centrum.json)
- Landmark outline 'Warsaw Unit' (41 points): bbox **x -2633..-2550, z 390..447** (includes the low base wings). Tower: **x -2633..-2563, z 399..447** (70 x 48 m, a parallelogram rotated ~20 deg).
- Building rows: base to 27 m; shaft **27 -> 168.8 m**; stepped top 168.8 -> 177.8 -> 180 m; the crown block **180 -> 202 m at x -2632..-2571, z 408..437** (and a 191 m step at x -2628..-2580, z 406..437). Real: 202 m, 46 storeys, 4-storey podium with a high glass hall/gate.
- Location: north-east side of Rondo Daszyńskiego, along Wolska/Prosta; the Rondo centre is about (-2698, 409), so the tower stands ~100 m east of it.

## Form and height
- A **tapered glass shaft** wider at the base than at the top (photo 2, photo 3: the faces lean in slightly, rooftop ~10 % smaller than the 27 m level), with sharp corners.
- At the top a **black crown block** (the Warta sign face, photo 2, 3, 4), 22 m tall, a plain box set flush on the shaft; slight lean/shift makes the top look like a cap (photo 3).
- Mid-height: a dark vertical band on each corner and a mid-face stripe (photo 3), so the face reads as three light glass strips divided by two black strips.
- The base (photo 4 from PKiN): a 4-storey, dark, sloped glass podium with a 2-storey open gate under the tower on the west; a glass entrance hall on the Rondo side; low connected wings to the east (the Warsaw Unit's "east wing", h 27 m).

## Facade
- Glass: **sky-blue reflective #6F99C2** in sun, **#2E4560** in shade (photo 3), spandrels dark **#1F2630**; module ~1.5 m wide, one glass pane per storey (storey ~4.1 m: 168.8 - 27 = 142 m / 35 floors above the base ~ 4.05 m).
- Dark vertical strips: charcoal-black **#20252C**, ~4.5 m wide at each corner and one centred strip on the long faces (photo 3).
- Silver frame lines at storey level are thin (~0.15 m); no fins, no balconies.

## Crown and sign
- Crown block: **black #1B1E22** panels, flat roof, red/white "Warta" logo on the long faces (photo 2, 3, 4), lit red at night in the real city (not photographed). The sign sits on the **long south-west face and the north-east face**, each ~9 m wide.
- Roof (ortho): crown box, plant boxes on the 168.8 / 177.8 m steps.

## Base and neighbours
- Rondo Daszyńskiego side: a glass hall, tram stop in front; metro entrance pavilion (red glass, photo 6) on the Rondo's corner; the Wolska tram line. Skyliner and the Hub are on the other side of the Rondo to the south-west; Towarowa 22 / GPY south of it (see gpy.md).
- Photo 5 shows the Rondo itself: a round green island with a cross of tram tracks and the metro pavilion at (-2705, 440).
