# Plac Bankowy's palaces (Ratusz, Urząd Wojewódzki, Giełda i Bank Polski) reference report
Files: refs/ratusz/ (9 photos: 2, 3, 5, 6 the colonnaded palace fronts; 7, 8 the Bank/Exchange rotunda; 9-11 the
Akcja pod Arsenałem boulder; ortho.jpg 260 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4,
z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md. Positions ±5 m.

- The **west side of Plac Bankowy** is one long front of Corazzi's palaces (2, 5): the **Pałac Komisji Rządowej
  Przychodów i Skarbu (today the city hall, Ratusz)** in the middle with a **portico of 6 giant Corinthian
  columns** and a pediment-less attic, flanked by two **wings with long colonnades of free-standing columns**
  (about 12 each) projecting to the square; the whole ~170 m, 3 storeys, **pale ochre-cream #E6D6AE** with white
  columns **#F2EEE3**, **green copper roofs #7DA894** (ortho: a ring of green roofs round courtyards).
- **Gmach Giełdy i Banku Polskiego** (7, 8): OSM centre (-1495, -894), on the square's south-west corner at
  Bielańska; a **rounded corner rotunda with a shallow green dome** (ortho: the round roof ~22 m across) and two
  storeys of **round-arched arcades** in two tiers, the lower rusticated, cream-ochre; partly a reconstructed
  ruin (the arcade wing).
- Słowacki's statue stands in front of the central portico (refs/slowacki); Starzyński at the square's north
  (refs/starzynski).
- **Akcja pod Arsenałem** memorial (9-11): a big grey granite boulder with the Kotwica in steel/stone and a
  plaque, flower beds, on the walk by a cream wall at Długa (the Arsenał); quiet card.
- Not covered: the Arsenał building's own front (no photo kept).
