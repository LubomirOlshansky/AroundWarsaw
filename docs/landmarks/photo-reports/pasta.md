# PAST-a (Zielna 37/39) reference report
Files: refs/pasta/ (3 photos: 1 the 1910s tower on Zielna, archive; 2 today from the south-west with Bank of
China next door; 3 the Uprising plaque; ortho.jpg 120 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±4 m.

- Position: OSM w27568865 centre (-1211, -255) (Mała PAST-a (-1199, -234) beside it); ortho: the **tower's roof
  ~25 x 25 m at about x -1232..-1205, z -268..-243**, its long shadow to the north-east; lower wings north and
  south along **Zielna** (the street on its east, x ~-1180).
- Tower (1, 2): **8-9 storeys, ~51 m**, square, brick-red / now grey-brown render **#8C7F72**, vertical pilaster
  strips, the top storey a **loggia of tall round arches (5 per face)** under a **crenellated parapet** (castle-
  like merlons); lower wings 5-6 storeys with steep roofs and tall chimneys (1). Today (2) flags and long white-red
  banners hang on it and a gilt emblem stands on the roof (an anchor-like sign).
- The plaque (3): "PASTA / 20 SIERPNIA 1944 ROKU BATALION KILIŃSKI ... ZDOBYŁ TEN GMACH" in white stone with the
  Virtuti cross, on the grey base. A quiet card, no gags (warsaw-tone).
