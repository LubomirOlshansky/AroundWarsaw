## Where it is
- The WKD terminus lies in a **narrow open cutting along the north kerb of Aleje Jerozolimskie**, between the
  walk and the big surface car park north of it (the Emilii Plater / Chałubińskiego block). OSM platform
  w157515015: centre (-1720, 740), about 187 m long, running WSW-ENE parallel to the avenue (about 17° north of
  east going east).
- The cutting (dark slot, 4-6 m wide, concrete retaining walls, a low railing on top) runs from about
  (-1785, 757) to (-1600, 690); it is **open in two pieces** with a deck between them at x -1730..-1712
  (a walkway / the crossing's landing) and goes **under a concrete deck east of x ~-1600** (the platform's
  covered end, photo 3) toward Emilii Plater (the road at x ~-1588).
- Platform (3): one island platform in the cut, under a low concrete slab on rows of round concrete columns
  along the wall; grey concrete, a yellow tactile strip, blue shop kiosk fronts, timetable screens; trains are
  WKD's red-white-blue EMUs (EN97). Two tracks.

## The two entrance pavilions (the "propeller" roofs) (photos 1, 2)
- West pavilion at the zebra/bike crossing: **(-1722, 728)** (±4 m), over the stair down from the north walk.
- East pavilion (2, "from the East"): **(-1632, 711)**, small box on the walk at the cutting's east open end.
- Shape (both alike): a free-standing concrete canopy of **two long tapering wings** (a boomerang / propeller
  in plan), each ~9-10 m long, ~3-4 m wide at the root narrowing to a rounded tip, the slab thin at the edge
  (~0.2 m) and thicker at the root, wing tips slightly raised; total span ~20 m, the slab ~3.8-4.2 m above
  the walk. It sits on **one sloping wedge-shaped pier** (raw concrete, wider at the top) set off-centre plus
  a second slim post under the other wing. Beneath: a waist-high white-painted concrete parapet box round the
  stair opening (graffiti on it), steel railings.
- Colours: canopy raw concrete warm grey-brown **#A39A8D** (underside darker #7E766B, edge lighter
  #B9B1A4); parapet white paint **#E6E4DF**; railings grey; sign: the **WKD logo** (red arrow over blue arrow in
  a white octagon, ~1 m) on the pier, and a long name board "WARSZAWA ŚRÓDMIEŚCIE WKD" (white on dark grey)
  on the west pavilion's wing.
- Around: black steel bollards, a Veturilo bike stand on the walk east of the east pavilion, the
  Jerozolimskie zebra with a red bike crossing (1).
