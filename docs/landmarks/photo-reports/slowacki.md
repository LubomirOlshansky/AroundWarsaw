# Pomnik Juliusza Słowackiego (Plac Bankowy) reference report
Files: refs/slowacki/ (3 photos, ortho.jpg 160 m square; the ortho is centred 61 m north of the statue, so the
statue sits 0.38 of the way below the centre). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±3 m.

- Position: OSM node **(-1480, -978)**, on the west side of Plac Bankowy in front of the arcaded yellow palace
  (Pałac Ministra Skarbu / Corazzi's palace, 1 and 3) and **facing away from it, east toward the square and
  Al. Solidarności / Senatorska** (the Błękitny Wieżowiec and Plac Bankowy's south-east appear behind it in 2).
- Form (1-3): a **dark grey-black granite pillar ~1.8 x 1.8 m, ~6 m high** with the name cut in its face, on a
  broad two-step granite platform (~12 x 10 m, dark grey **#4A4A4C**); on top the poet standing, wrapped in a long
  cloak, head down, **dark bronze #33342F**, ~4.5 m. Total ~11 m.
- Behind it: the palace's ground-floor arcade (cream **#EADFC2**, white columns), lamp posts with two lanterns on
  the platform's corners.
