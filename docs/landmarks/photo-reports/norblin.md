# Fabryka Norblina: trees, court and roofs (K6 check) reference report
Files: refs/norblin/ (6 photos: 1-3 the museum machines inside, 4 archive, 5-6 the street fronts before the
rebuild; none shows the court's trees, so the trees below are from ortho.jpg only, 220 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±3 m.

- The complex lies between Żelazna (east, x ~-2180) and Prosta (south, z ~240-260); the **glass-roofed internal
  street** runs diagonally (WSW-ENE) from about (-2300, 230) to (-2195, 190), ~6 m wide.
- **The court with the big planes**: a tree cluster of **3-4 broad crowns (~7-9 m across each)** at
  **x -2239..-2225, z 173..190**, in the open yard north of the glass street between the old halls, with café
  tables round them; this is where the map's 5 candelabra planes should stand (check they are not under a roof).
- More trees: a row of 3-4 young trees along Żelazna at x ~-2188, z 203..226; small trees in planters along the
  glass street's south side (x -2280..-2220, z 225..235); a **small garden court** in the west block at
  x -2316..-2305, z 205..218.
- **Roofs**: several green (sedum) roofs and roof terraces on the new blocks: the north-west block's terraces
  with small trees/planters at x -2290..-2275, z 140..160 and x -2315..-2300, z 135..150 (the "garden terraces
  upstairs"), many roof plant rooms (grey).
- Climbers: not readable from above; no photo. Leave the walls bare (default).
