# Dom Towarowy Braci Jabłkowskich (Bracka 25) reference report
Files: refs/jablkowskich/ (5 photos: 5 the whole front; 1, 3 facade details; 2 the entrance columns; 4 the
stained-glass windows inside; ortho.jpg 120 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±5 m.

- Position: OSM w28823842 centre (-589, 211); ortho: the block **x -618..-560, z 182..233** on the west side of
  **Bracka** (the paved woonerf east of it, with young trees in planters), a **glazed atrium roof** in the middle
  (x -600..-575, z 200..220).
- Front (5): **6 storeys + an attic storey**, ~28 m; **warm beige-ochre stone render #D9C3A0** with a grey-brown
  stone ground floor **#7D6B5A**; a 2-storey base of **round-arched shop windows** (about 9) behind slim piers;
  a balustraded balcony band over the 2nd storey (3: stone balusters with lion-mask roundels between them);
  regular rows of tall windows with small decorative panels (1: an art-nouveau mask and acanthus relief in the
  spandrels); a cartouche with the founders' initials on the cornice. The current shop name is a real brand: parody it.
- Entrance (2): two fat white Doric columns flanking a door with art-nouveau ironwork in the glazing.
- Inside (4): three huge **round-arched stained-glass windows** (art nouveau, figures and flowers) in the
  atrium wall: a lit-at-night detail visible through the front (optional).
