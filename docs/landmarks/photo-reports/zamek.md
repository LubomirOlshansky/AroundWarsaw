# Royal Castle (Zamek Królewski) reference report
Files: refs/zamek/ (13 Commons photos 01-13 + ortho.jpg 3072x2603; SOURCES.md has licences). Not examined: krajobraz.com.pl (not opened, nothing kept from it).
Game coords: x=(lon-21.024)*68240.4, z=(52.234-lat)*110574. Ortho: x=-760+px*0.1237 (full px) ; z=-1720+py*0.1237 (8.08 px/m). Values below are from the 2000-px preview: x=-760+0.19*px, z=-1720+0.19*py.
All ortho numbers are eyeballed, about +-4 m. Towers lean in the ortho (caps shifted off their bases).

## Castle, orientation
- Plan = irregular pentagon around the Great Courtyard; the long west wing (facing Plac Zamkowy) is the west block, outer wall x~-676, z -1564..-1471 (~95 m). Sigismund column at x~-728, z~-1469 (square lies W and SW of the castle).
- Vistula (east) wing is the long diagonal block from N end (x~-627, z~-1629) to S tip (x~-595, z~-1460), ~165 m, axis tilted ~13 deg from N-S (top leans west). North block (z -1629..-1568, x -640..-600) is narrower (~25 m).
- Overall castle bbox: x -676..-585, z -1629..-1460.
- Clock Tower (Wieża Zygmuntowska) at the north end of the west wing, base ~x -667, z -1564, flush with the west front, square shaft ~9x9 m, ~60 m tall (shaft ~38 m, helmet+spire ~22 m).
- Other green-capped corner turrets (ortho): x -667,z -1564 (clock tower); x -670,z -1516 (west wing mid, small); x -671,z -1473 (SW corner); x -598,z -1517 and -596,z -1516 (east wing, courtyard side, two); S tip turret (Grodzka/stair tower) x -595,z -1460. Each ~4x4 m, 28-32 m high.
- Central green oval copper dome of the Vistula facade at x -596, z -1568 (~17 m wide).

## Colours (photo estimates)
- West front (and courtyard east/south wings, tower shaft, turrets): salmon-red plaster **#C9766A** (overcast #B8604F, sunny #D98672); stone dressings cream **#E8DCC0**: corner quoins (alternating long/short, ~1 m wide), plain window surrounds, plinth band, cornice, dentil frieze under the tower helmet (gilt dentils #D8B24A).
- Roofs: terracotta tile **#C0502E** (lit #C85A3A, shade #9E3E2A), steep gabled/hipped, ~35-40 deg, small gabled dormers in rows, cream chimneys (#E8E0D0) every ~12 m along the ridge. Main ranges: west block, north block, east wing: all one tile roof.
- Clock Tower: shaft salmon (#C9766A) with cream quoin strips each side, cornice with cream brackets, gilt dentils, iron-railed balcony ~2/3 up; green copper onion helmet **#5E9A7E** (patina, darker #4C8268 at seams), two small dormers with louvres on the bulb, lantern + second smaller onion, thin spire, gilt ball #D8B24A and small weather-flag on top. Clock faces: dark navy/black disc **#1E2A3A** with gold numerals and a gold sun, two faces visible (west and south), set in a cream-framed recess, at ~ 1/3 shaft height from top of shaft.
- Corner turrets: same salmon shaft, small bulbous copper cap #6FA58A with lantern and spire, gilt ball.
- Entrance gate (west front, 1/3 from tower end): single round-arched portal ~4 m wide x 5 m high, cream rusticated surround with a small cartouche above; dark opening; steps/ramp, no door visible (open passage).
- Pavements: Plac Zamkowy = large grey-brown granite slabs **#8E8076** (01), 4x2 m, in wide joints.

## West front (Plac Zamkowy)
- Length ~95 m. 3 storeys read from the street (ground + 2 upper, upper floor with taller windows), windows cream-framed, rectangular, dark panes, ~18 bays (counted ~16-19 in 01/04), regular rhythm; every ~3rd storey band has a thin cream string course. Plain wall, little ornament; a gentle plinth in stone ~1 m. No balcony.
- Roof ridge height above ground ~ 24-26 m total; eaves ~ 16-17 m.

## Courtyard (Dziedziniec Wielki)
- Irregular pentagon, inner size ~ 45 x 40 m. Vertices (game): NW (-656,-1547), NE (-612,-1547), E (-605,-1518), SE (-609,-1487), SW (-656,-1487).
- Paving: cobble setts **#B0A590** laid in fan/arc pattern (06), with a lighter grey granite cross-walk **#A9ADAD** (bands ~3 m wide) from the gate, crossing the centre (axes x~-635 N-S and z~-1523 E-W in ortho).
- Courtyard walls: east/north side facing yard = pinkish-beige plaster **#D2B49A** with cream pilaster strips, 3 storeys, wrought balcony (iron railing) on first floor, central sculpted roof gable with statues, tall dormers (06). South/west wings in the yard = salmon #C9766A with cream surrounds.
- Several small shed dormers in the yard roof (white-framed), turrets visible on the corners.

## Vistula (east) facade (03, 08, 09, 13)
- Cream-white plaster **#EDE6D6** over a pinkish-grey rusticated ground floor **#BFA89A**; window surrounds light stone **#F4EEDD**; grey-green shutters on some windows.
- 3 storeys + red-tile mansard roof with arched dormers. ~26-30 bays total (about 11 left of the left side risalit, risalit group, about 9 right).
- Layout left to right (viewed from the river): plain wing - gabled side risalit (triangular pediment with cartouche/statues, ~3 bays) - 4-arch loggia on ground (open round arches) with balustraded balcony above - **central oval risalit** (curved bay, ~17 m wide, 3 storeys, convex, big oval windows, gilt statues on top corners and clock roundel) with copper dome **#6E9E88** carrying gilt statue groups and cartouches - loggia of 4 arches - second gabled side risalit - plain wing. Side risalits project ~2 m and are ~14 m wide; spacing between the risalit centres ~ 28 m. Attic on risalits: curved/sculpted, small statues at corners (gilt look).
- Terrace in front of the facade: cobbled **#B5AB98**, ~12 m wide, with a long row of tubs (ball-topped maples on bare ~2.5 m stems, crowns ~1.3 m wide) in chevron-painted wooden barrels (red #B8473A / dark green #4C6B4A / white #F2F0E8), spaced ~ 4 m, along both terrace edges. Box-hedge knot beds at the foot of the facade.

## Kubicki Arcades (07, 03, 12; ortho)
- Terrace edge / wall line: from (-584,-1675) to (-515,-1489) in ortho (N end near the Ogród Górny's north corner, S end at the Pałac pod Blachą side), length ~200 m, ~15 deg off N-S (leans east going south).
- Face towards the Lower Garden: terracotta-orange plaster **#CC6A40** (03: #C4543B), cream stone arch rings, quoins and cornice **#E5DCC0**, ~7-8 m high wall, large round arches (~6 m wide x 5 m high, black glazed or open) every ~12 m, with round oculi between arches. Centre: a white stone block with big arched portal (7 m wide) and two diagonal stair flights climbing left and right to the terrace; iron railings black on the terrace edge (#222), 1 m high.
- Ramps/stairs from the ortho (±6 m): portal block near (-554,-1611) with the dark opening; stair block along the wall near (-538,-1572); small stair/ramp at the S end (-525,-1500).

## Upper Garden (Ogród Górny) on/above the arcades (ortho)
- Strip between the castle's east wing and the arcades wall: N corner (-601,-1666), S end near (-535,-1490); width ~25-40 m (widest mid), area ~ 55-60% lawn.
- Parterre: two diagonal gravel paths crossing like an X (from (-614,-1650) to (-560,-1536), and (-595,-1627) to (-540,-1560)), light grey-beige **#CFCBC0**, width ~2 m, round nodes at (-596,-1623) and (-560,-1536) (~6 m dia); long straight path along the arcades wall edge (~3 m wide) and a main N-S path along the castle foot.
- Lawn panels (box-hedge framed, **#6AA841**, hedge frame #3E7A2C ~0.4 m): NW series at about (-591,-1663), (-585,-1648), (-578,-1627), (-568,-1606), each ~8x13 m; SE series at (-553,-1572), (-545,-1544), (-536,-1520), (-526,-1505), each ~8x13 m; larger triangular open lawn between wing and path (x~-590..-570, z -1613..-1530).
- Two fountains: round bowl fountain with stepped plaza at **(-557,-1585)** (fountain basin ~5 m dia, stone grey, steps down on the E side, flower-shaped tulip bowl in 09); small round fountain/well at **(-585,-1574)** near the wing foot (~3 m dia).
- Row of tubs along the terrace edge: tubs at ~4 m spacing, along the castle foot from (-588,-1580) to (-577,-1520) (see Vistula terrace above), and on the arcades-edge side the black iron railing.
- Ornamental baroque embroidered parterre (yellow-brown/box scrollwork, **#B89A52** gravel + #3E7A2C box) at the castle's SE corner: x -581..-568, z -1522..-1492 (~13x30 m). This is the "south quarter garden"; lawn/hedge strip at (-613..-600,-1465).

## Lower Garden (Ogród Dolny), east of the arcades wall (ortho)
- Extent: x -563..-437, z -1693..-1530 on the north half (mazes), and the lawn/paving apron x -540..-440, z -1630..-1560, closed to the east by the Wisłostrada embankment hedge (x ~ -445) with the Wisłostrada road beyond (x>-440).
- Two square box-hedge labyrinth quarters: left (x -562..-511, z -1675..-1632) and right (x -516..-469, z -1690..-1646), each ~50x45 m, hedge ~1 m high, **#4F8A33** (sunlit #5C9B3A), passages ~1.5 m, with a fountain/statue at the centre (left centre at (-538,-1654); right at (-493,-1676)); flowerbeds (yellow/red) in the corridor between them (x~-513).
- Sunken lawn carpet **#6AA841**: x -531..-462, z -1628..-1589, rectangle with semicircular east end (centre (-480,-1608), radius ~18 m), sunk ~1 m with corner steps (granite grey #C8C4BA) at the west side; cobbled/granite paving **#C8C4BA** all round (~10 m wide), white stone vases on pedestals every ~17 m along both long sides.
- Rows of clipped hedge bars (~15 m long x 1.5 m) in a diagonal fan south of the lawn (x -530..-470, z -1610..-1560), and an L-shaped hedge boscket at x -523..-500, z -1570..-1526 (hedge #3E7A2C).
- Main axis: castle central oval risalit (-596,-1568) -> arcade portal (-554,-1611)... the garden's own axis is E-W along the lawn centreline z ~ -1608 from the wall to x -462; 09/03 show a 5-6 m long lawn centre with white stone seats, no canal. No canal found; fountain only in the maze centres and the upper garden.
- Colour: lawn #6AA841, gravel/paving light grey-beige #CFCBC0, hedge #3E7A2C-#5C9B3A, vases/steps white-grey #E6E3DA.

## Pałac pod Blachą and the south end
- Palace pod Blachą: hipped roof of pale green copper **#8DBFA3**, x -536..-511, z -1477..-1454 (~25x23 m), white/cream walls **#E8E4D8**, 2 storeys + cornice, balustraded attic with statues; attached long wings with grey-green low roofs (#7A9A92) at x -590..-525, z -1491..-1440, forming a paved court (dark grey stone, #6B6F72) at x -586..-537, z -1474..-1456.
- Brown flat/green roof strip at x -588..-523, z -1491..-1480 (arcade-top lawn).
- South lawn terraces ("kwatery", stepped lawns with hedge lines) between the castle's south front and Podwale/Wisłostrada: x -682..-615, z -1458..-1429, 3-4 stripes of lawn **#7FA04E** separated by hedges #3E7A2C; small garden hut at x -675,z -1438.
- Wisłostrada/Karowa road below at z ~-1430..-1405.

## Courtyard approach notes for the model
- Roof is the identity: one continuous terracotta pentagon ring, ridge ~ 24-26 m above ground, many small dormers; Clock Tower + green onion caps; the Vistula facade is cream-white (clearly lighter than the salmon west front); arcades below it in orange-red with arches; gardens as listed.
