- Position: OSM w243189987 **(-2382, -2020)**, north side of ul. Stawki at Dzika, by the tram tracks on Stawki.
- Form (1, 4): an open-topped **rectangular enclosure of white-grey marble slabs** (light **#DADAD6**, slabs ~1 x 2 m),
  ~4 m high, ~16 m along the street, ~10 m deep, open to the sky. In the middle of the street side a **gate**: a
  tall narrow opening crowned by a **black stone semicircle** with a relief of broken trees; a black granite band
  runs round the outside at about 2/3 height with "UMSCHLAGPLATZ" cut in it (4). Inside: names on the walls.
