# Hotel Warszawa (the 1934 Prudential) reference report
Files: refs/hotel_warszawa/ (6 photos: 1 night, 2 the restored front, 3 and 6 the bare post-war shell during
the rebuild, 4-5 archive 1960s; ortho.jpg 160 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±4 m.

- Position: OSM w697649358 centre (-797, -182), west side of **Plac Powstańców Warszawy** (the paved square with
  lawns east of it, x -775..-735, z -230..-120). The **tower stands on the square's side**: its roof at about
  x -782..-765, z -199..-178 (~17 x 21 m); the lower wings fill the block west of it to x ~-830 (a glazed
  courtyard roof in the middle, ortho).
- Form (2): a **stepped tower, 17 storeys, ~66 m**: a 13-storey main shaft with 7 bays on the front, then a
  **set-back 3-storey top** (narrower by one bay each side) with a flat roof and three thin masts; on each side a
  **6-storey wing** about as wide as 3 bays, flush with the front. Vertical **pilaster strips** between the window
  columns run the full height, windows small and square-ish, deep set; a 2-storey base with five tall openings
  framed in dark red stone (**#7E3B33**) at the entrance.
- Colour (2, restored 2018): **pale limestone / travertine #E7E0D1** (shade #CBC3B1), window frames bronze-grey
  **#5E5A52**. Night (1): warm floodlight washing up the pilasters (#F5C77A), the crown brightest.
- The neighbours on the square: the 5-storey neo-classical block south (1, cream #DCCBAF).
