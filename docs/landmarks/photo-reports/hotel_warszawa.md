# Hotel Warszawa (the 1933 Prudential) reference report

Files: refs/hotel_warszawa/ (best: 2 front elevation 2021, 7 and 8 corner views 2020/2024, 1 night 2019, ortho.jpg; 3-6, 9-12 historical or under rebuild). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Ortho frame in SOURCES.md. Positions +-4 m. Written from the photos on F's Mac 2026-11-02.

## Identity (what makes a passer-by say its name)
1. A tall **stepped stone tower**: a wide main shaft, one big projecting cornice, then a narrower square crown box with a second cornice and thin flagpoles/masts on the roof.
2. **Vertical pilaster strips** (full height, 8 across the front) between columns of small, deep-set, square-ish windows: the whole shaft reads as vertical stripes, no horizontal bands except the two cornices.
3. Pale **sandstone / limestone cladding in blocks** (warm cream, with a few orange-ochre stained blocks).
4. A **low, heavy 2-storey base** with a 3-opening portal and the grey incised lettering "WARSZAWA", flanked by **copper-red doors/panels**.
5. **Two flat-topped wings** as tall as ~6 storeys flush with the front, each with one column of tall narrow window slits.

## Massing
- Block: x about -853..-755, z about -200..-116 (ortho; ~98 x 84 m) west of Plac Powstańców Warszawy (x -755 and east: paved square, lawns, a stepped seating area, a Metro/bus structure), south of Świętokrzyska (the street running ENE along the north side at z about -203). South neighbour: the 5-storey cream TVP block (Plac Powstańców 7).
- **Tower**: roof at x -783..-766 (17 m E-W), z -199..-178 (21 m N-S), so it stands at the NE corner of the block, nearest the plac and Świętokrzyska. The entrance front faces EAST onto the plac, width 21 m, depth 17 m. Height about 66 m (LoD2 ~70), 16-17 storeys: ground floor (taller, ~5 m) + 1 low storey of small windows (the base) + **12 storeys shaft** + **2 storeys crown** + attic/cornice. Storey ~3.8 m.
- **Crown box**: set back on every side by about one half-bay (~2.5 m), 5 window columns on the front (shaft has 7), 2 rows of windows, top cornice ~2 m tall projecting ~1 m, flat roof. Three flagpoles/masts (2 thin white masts plus flagpoles with white-red flags), 6-8 m tall, on the roof.
- **Two 6-storey wings** (flat roof, ~22-25 m, LoD2 25 m): one north and one south of the shaft, each about 6 m wide on the front, flush with the front plane, the top of the wing ending at about the 6th storey of the shaft (just above the 5th row of shaft windows counted from the bottom). The south wing meets the TVP block; the north wing turns the corner into the Świętokrzyska frontage.
- **Long north wing** along Świętokrzyska: ~6 storeys (about 22-25 m), roughly 75 m long running west from the tower, plain stone, 3-4 window rows of small square windows with a continuous cornice (photo 7 right side, photo 10). Its roof (ortho, west part, bright flat roof) is tall; a glazed courtyard/atrium roof (blue-green, curved end, plant boxes) fills the middle of the block between the wings.
- **Lower south/east part** (the dark roofs in the ortho, z -165..-120): about 8-10 m, with a rounded SE corner onto the plac (about x -756, z -122) and a small pyramid skylight; plant and fans on the dark roof.
- Footprint, front plane and wings are almost symmetric around the portal.

## Front facade (east, onto the plac), base to top
- **Ground floor** (~5 m): centre **portal**, 3 tall openings (1 wide centre between piers) with deep square stone piers; grey incised lettering "WARSZAWA" (about 5 m wide, letters ~0.4 m) above the portal on the stone lintel; left and right of the portal one **narrow copper-red door** (#A5583F) set into a stone surround; dark glazed doors/lobby. Forecourt: shallow stone steps and a stone seating/ramp composition (photo 2 foreground), 3 flagpoles at the right side.
- **Base storey 2**: a row of 7 small square deep windows (width ~1.3 m) under a plain stone band.
- **Shaft**: **8 full-height pilasters** (about 0.6 m wide, projecting ~0.3 m) with chamfered/stepped profile; between them **7 columns** of windows, **12 rows**: window ~1.4 m wide x 1.7 m tall, deep reveal ~0.3 m, a plain stone panel (spandrel) between rows, grey-teal glass, light curtains behind. Pilaster strips run unbroken base to cornice. At the corners the outer strips are doubled/stepped (photo 8: 3-4 stepped narrow strips on the side faces).
- **Cornice** above the shaft (the setback): heavy flat slab ~1.5 m tall projecting ~1 m, then the crown box.
- **Crown**: 5 columns x 2 rows of the same windows, 6 pilasters, plain attic and thin projecting flat top cornice (red-brown tile band under it is only visible pre-restoration, photo 11; hidden now).
- **Wings (front)**: each is plain stone, one column of tall narrow windows (about 1.1 x 2.4 m, 5 stacked) near the outer edge; one small square window at the base level. Top: flat with a thin dark roof edge.
- **Side faces** (north and south) of the shaft: same system, 6 columns, the pilasters doubled at the edges; SE/NE corner of the 6-storey wing has a corner of **tall glazed slits** (photo 8: stacked tall narrow glass at the wing corner next to the entrance).

## Corners
Sharp (square) on the tower and wings. Only the low south-east part of the block has a rounded corner (ortho, radius ~8 m).

## Roof (ortho and photos)
- Tower roof: small flat roof, dark grey, with a stair/lift head and a skylight (ortho at x -778, z -190); masts/flags above.
- Wings and long north wing: flat, light grey-white membrane (the long west wing roof is pale #CFCBC4), parapet 0.5 m, a few round plant hoods.
- Courtyard in the middle: glazed roof in two parts (a long ridge strip along the north side and a curved-end hall to the south), blue-green glass #7F9FAF, dark plant platforms with a row of fans (x -835..-790, z -160..-125).
- Dark low roofs of the lower part: tar #55504A with a pyramid skylight at about (-775, -140).

## Colours (daylight photos 2, 7, 8)
- Stone, sunlit: #E3D6BC (pale cream, photo 2 reads #E8E0D0 in overcast); in shade #CBBFA8; ochre-stained blocks #D9B98A (sprinkled, about 1 in 12).
- Pilasters: same stone, slightly lighter edge #EDE4D0; spandrel panels between window rows slightly darker #D8CDB6.
- Glass: #6F7F86 (grey-teal), bright sky reflections #8FA6B5; window frames bronze-grey #5E5A52; curtains cream.
- Ground floor: stone #DDD0B6; portal reveal dark #3C3733; copper-red doors/panels #A5583F; lettering #8A857B (incised, no colour).
- Roof: #55504A (tar), #CFCBC4 (bright membrane), courtyard glass #7F9FAF.
- Flags: white and red; masts white.

## Signs and lettering
Only "WARSZAWA" in the stone over the portal (grey, incised, capital letters ~0.4 m). Hotel brand names not visible on the facade (restored 2018 as hotel Warszawa Prudential; a parody name could use "WARSZAWA"). In the 2019 photo no signs on the tower. The TVP neighbour carries a roof sign "POLSKA SA" (not part of this building).

## Night (photo 1)
**Floodlights wash up the pilasters** (the strips glow warm yellow #F7CD6E, window columns stay dark), the two cornices are outlined by a bright warm line, the crown glows the brightest; a thin lit line at the crown top. Windows mostly dark, a few warm (a hotel: some #F5C77A). Ground floor: warm lit lobby glass at the portal; the glazed pavilion at the south-east foot is lit. Street lights warm #FFB75E.

## Ground (what stands at its foot)
- East: Plac Powstańców Warszawy: pale granite paving, a stepped seating/ramp composition with planters in front of the portal, flat lawn strips running N-S (x -770..-740), a few small trees; a tram/bus road further east (x about -735).
- North: Świętokrzyska with a red cycle path, a row of street trees (plane/lime), cars parked at the kerb; tram-less.
- A low glazed hall and rounded corner at the SE foot. South: the TVP block, an alley, parking.
- Street furniture: black bollards (#2A2A2A, ~0.9 m) along the plac kerb, signposts, 3 flagpoles by the portal.
