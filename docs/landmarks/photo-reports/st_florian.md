# Katedra św. Michała Archanioła i św. Floriana (Praga) reference report
Files: refs/st_florian/ (3 photos: 4 the west front by day, 5 by night, 6 from afar with the nave; ortho.jpg 160 m). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±6 m (centres from OSM/Nominatim).
- Position: centre **(467, -1964)**, on Aleja Solidarności's north side by Floriańska; the **west front with the
  two spires faces south-west onto Al. Solidarności** (the paved forecourt), the nave runs north-east behind it
  (ortho: steep dark grey-green slate roofs, the towers' copper tops casting long shadows).
- Form (4, 6): neo-Gothic **red brick #A4472E** with white/cream stone trim **#E9E3D3** (pinnacles, window
  tracery, string courses); **two square towers ~75 m to the cross**: brick shafts with tall lancet openings, a
  white-trimmed belfry, then **tall octagonal green copper spires #5E8E76** with lucarnes and gilt crosses; between
  them a steep gable with a rose window and a pointed central portal (three portals in all, 4); a slender flèche
  over the crossing; the nave roof dark slate **#4A524F** with small turrets at the transept corners (6).
- Night (5): floodlit warm orange, spires lit cool cyan. A white statue (St Michael) on the forecourt (5).
