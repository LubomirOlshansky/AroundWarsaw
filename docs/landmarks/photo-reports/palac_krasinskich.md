# Pałac Krasińskich (Pałac Rzeczypospolitej) reference report
Files: refs/palac_krasinskich/ (5 photos: 3, 5 the garden front from Ogród Krasińskich; 6, 7, 8 the city front
and its pediment; ortho.jpg 220 m square, but **read the position off refs/krasinskich/ortho.jpg**, where the
palace is fully in frame). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±5 m.

- Position: the long block with **green copper roofs** at about **x -1189..-1098, z -1716..-1667**, its long
  axis running NW-SE (about 35° off E-W), **the city front facing south-west onto Plac Krasińskich** (the
  Uprising monument's square) and **the garden front facing north-east onto Ogród Krasińskich**.
- Form (3, 5, 7, 8): a **baroque palace ~95 m long, 3 storeys** with a **central risalit crowned by a big
  triangular pediment full of sculpted reliefs** (white-grey stone), statues on the pediment's apex and corners,
  a balustraded attic along the roof edge with urns; the ground floor with **round-arched windows** (dark green
  frames on the city side, 7), upper floors with segmental pediments over the windows; two **side risalits** with
  smaller pediments; a mansard-like **green copper roof #6E9C82** with white chimneys and dormers.
- Colours: walls **white #F1EFEA** (city front, 7-8) to warm cream **#E8DFC9** (garden front, 3); stone dressings
  pale grey; the main door dark red-brown wood **#7A3A22** under a balcony.
- Garden side (3, 5): the parterre with flower beds (red, white), lawns, paths and old trees right up to the front.
