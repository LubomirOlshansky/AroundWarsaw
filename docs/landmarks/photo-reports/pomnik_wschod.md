# Pomnik Poległym i Pomordowanym na Wschodzie reference report
Files: refs/pomnik_wschod/ (4 photos, ortho.jpg 140 m square centred on OSM). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±3 m.

- Position: OSM w244832050 **(-1718, -2184)**, in the **wide median of ul. Muranowska** at Al. Jana Pawła II /
  General Andersa (ortho: a cobbled median strip x -1765..-1690, z -2195..-2175 between two carriageways).
- Form (1-4): a **bronze railway flatcar on rails** (~7 m long, ~2.5 m wide) piled with a dense thicket of
  **crosses** (Latin and Orthodox), with a Jewish matzevah and a Muslim tombstone among them, up to ~6 m above
  the street; it stands at the **east end of a track of ~41 bronze sleepers** (each ~2.6 m long, names of places
  in raised letters, 2) laid on a slightly raised **mound of rounded field stones** (grey-pink **#8E8478**) that runs
  ~37 m west from the wagon. Bronze dark green-brown **#3E3F33** with lighter verdigris.
- The wagon faces east (the track behind it to the west). A bronze plaque in the cobbles (3).
- Respectful treatment as for Umschlagplatz.
