# Pomnik Małego Powstańca reference report
Files: refs/maly_powstaniec/ (4 photos, ortho.jpg 100 m square centred on OSM). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±3 m.

- Position: OSM node **(-981, -1689)**, ul. Podwale, against the **inner face of the Old Town's red-brick city wall**
  (ortho: the wall runs NNE-SSW here, a lawn strip and the outer wall to the west, the Podwale houses east).
- Form (1-3): a small bronze boy (~1.4 m) in a far-too-big helmet (painted white-red band) and a long coat, a
  submachine gun on a strap, on a **narrow dark stone post ~0.5 x 0.5 x 1.3 m**; behind him a **semicircular
  brick terrace** (~4 m wide, low brick walls ~0.6 m, red-orange brick **#B5573C** with grey cobbles set in) with a
  bronze plaque on the wall, a black iron railing on the terrace's back edge, red and white flower beds, grave
  candles. Bronze **#4A4C48**.
