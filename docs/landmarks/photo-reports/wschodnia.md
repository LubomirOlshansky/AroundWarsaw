# Warszawa Wschodnia reference report (railway S3e; mostly off the map's edge)
Files: refs/wschodnia/ (3 older photos named by title, 6 new numbered 1-6; 2, 3, 5 are interiors; ortho.jpg
380 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-01/02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±5 m.

- OSM station node (1936, -1945). In the ortho: the **main hall** is the big flat-roofed block on the north side of
  the tracks, about **x 1814..1963, z -2057..-1966**, its long axis parallel to the tracks (the tracks run
  WSW-ENE, ~25° north of east going east); a bus/taxi forecourt and car park north of it (Kijowska side).
- **Platforms**: 5 island platforms south-east of the hall, x 1860..2120, with long **rust-brown roofs**
  **#8E5A44** (ortho) on rows of columns; under-track passages connect them to the hall and to the Lubelska
  side (south).
- Hall front (1, 2018 refit): a long **glazed front under a folded (zig-zag) concrete roof edge** carried on
  **V-shaped steel struts** (white-grey at the Kijowska front, **pale green #9CC38E** on the Lubelska side, 4),
  glass curtain walls, white letters **WARSZAWA WSCHODNIA** on the dark roof fascia; a low concrete barrier row on
  the forecourt.
- Inside (2, 3, 5): grey granite floor, steel lattice roof with glazed strips, shops (use parody names only).
- Only the hall's west end and the tracks reach the map; default: the edge ring as now plus the hall's west end.
