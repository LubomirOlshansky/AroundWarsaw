# Warsaw Uprising Monument (Pomnik Powstania Warszawskiego, Wincenty Kućma 1989) reference report
Files: refs/pomnik_powstania/ (14 Commons photos 01-14 + ortho.jpg 3072x3072, SOURCES.md has licences). No true aerial and no clean night photo exist on Commons: 06 (dusk, lit) and 07 (night, 1 Aug candles) are the night refs.
Game coords: x=(lon-21.024)*68240.4 (111320*cos 52.234), z=(52.234-lat)*110574. Ortho: x = -1510 + px/7.314, z = -1900 + py/7.314. Ortho numbers are eyeballed, about +-2 m (the monument is low, so it does not lean much). Heights are NOT measured, they are estimated from the photos against people (see "Heights").
Compass: bearing from north, clockwise. Game direction for bearing b: dx = sin b, dz = -cos b (z is south).

## Layout: two bronze groups, 25 m apart, on the NW corner of Plac Krasińskich
Both groups stand in front of the Supreme Court (Sąd Najwyższy, green-glass colonnade) and its raised terrace, which closes the square on the north and west side.
1. **"Szturmujący" (the Storming Insurgents, the big group)**: at the foot of the Supreme Court, on top of a wide flight of steps. OSM way 1327606552 (name Pomnik Powstania Warszawskiego), polygon bbox x -1243..-1222, z -1706..-1693, centre (-1235,-1700); the polygon is the bronze+slab footprint, minimum rectangle 18.5 x 14.3 m at 48 deg. That outline is right (checked on ortho). Ortho slab positions: left slab/pillar (top face a light parallelogram) roughly x -1245..-1238, z -1706..-1692; right slab x -1239..-1228, z -1704..-1693; bronze wall between and below them, fighters along the front (SE) edge.
2. **"Przejście kanałami" (Descent into the sewers)**: a small group on the paved square's west edge, next to the juniper/tree bed. OSM has only a 4x5 m war_memorial way 1449265995 at x -1236..-1232, z -1676..-1671 (centre -1234,-1674) with no name. Ortho: dark figure clusters at about (-1236.5,-1672) and (-1236.5,-1676.5), a light slab-top at (-1236,-1676.5), group length ~8 m along the bed edge (NNW-SSE, z -1678..-1669), depth ~3 m (x -1239..-1233 at the bed edge). The edge of the paving there runs NW-SE at bearing ~148 deg.
   Plaque nodes: "Przejście Kanałami do Śródmieścia" (-1235,-1620), "Batalion Pięść" (-1238,-1610) are Tchorek-style wall plaques 55 m south on Długa, not part of the group.
3. Distance between the groups: (-1235,-1700) to (-1234,-1674) = 26 m, nearly straight N-S. The "Szturmujący" is north, "kanały" south.

## "Szturmujący" group (photos 01-06, 14)
- **What it is**: a ruined brick wall (bronze, brick courses, broken out) with two huge tilted light-granite slabs/pillars leaning over it; ~11 fighters in bronze run out of the wall toward the square. Left slab is the short-high one, right slab the long one; both are rectangular boxes tilted ~35-40 deg from vertical, their lower edge resting on the bronze wall. Between and below them a dark bronze relief of vertical rectangular blocks (the "rubble city") fills the gap (photos 02, 04, 05).
- **Facing**: the fighters face SSE onto the steps and square, about bearing 150-160 deg (game direction (+0.4,+0.9)). The steps' risers run parallel to the Supreme Court wall at bearing ~67 deg (WSW-ENE, ortho: wall from (-1275,-1697) to (-1240,-1712)), so the group front is perpendicular to that, bearing 157 deg.
- **Steps**: wide flight in front of the group (05, 06): ~9-10 treads, ~20 m wide, ~12 m deep along the run, total rise ~1.5 m (est), light granite treads #D1D2CD in the ortho with thin dark joints every ~1.6 m. Zone in ortho: x -1262..-1240, z -1704..-1685. The group stands on the top landing, bronze figures along the front edge at z -1700..-1695.
- **Slabs**: footprint of each ~5 x 11 m (left) and ~6 x 13 m (right) in plan, tilted; sides plain light granite #C5C7BA (lit) .. #878D8B (shade), top face lighter #D6D8D3 (ortho). Underside smooth.
- **Fighters** (figures run from the wall): bronze, about 2.2-2.5 m (bigger than life; a fighter beside the lowest brick course reaches roughly the slab foot). Two run left (west) at the group edge, ~5 in the central relief (one kneeling with rifle), 2 right at the far end of the long slab, one with rifle at the extreme right (03, 04).

## "Przejście kanałami" group (photos 08, 09, 10)
- A bronze priest (standing, right side, ~3 m with plinth, robe, hands joined, bareheaded) with 2-3 soldiers bent over the sewer manhole (one with Stahlhelm leaning down, one kneeling, one half in the manhole), on a low rectangular stone block of cobble-like stacked stones (~2.5 x 1.5 m, ~0.8 m high) (08, 09). A second stepped broken-slab sculpture (grey-green bronze, ~3 x 2 m, ~0.5 m high, 10) lies in the juniper bed in front, the "sewer cover" debris.
- Faces: the figures face SE/E, into the square, bearing ~110-150 deg (est, camera on the square sees them from the front). The manhole sits at the front (square) side of the block.
- Figures ~2.2-2.5 m kneeling/standing, priest ~2.8 m.

## Heights (estimates)
- Overall: slab tops ~8-9 m above the top landing (right slab tallest; slabs span roughly 4x the height of a fighter plus the wall: fighters 2.3 m, wall ~3.5 m, slab top ~8.5 m). I could not find a published height; treat +-1.5 m.
- Bronze wall/relief ~5-6 m. Group top landing ~1.5 m above the square.
- Supreme Court behind: OSM column boxes height 14 m (marble, #deeded) and the glass block above them; the green colonnade roof band is ~14 m, glass volume above that to ~25 m.
- Sewer group: block 0.8 m, figures to 2.5-3 m. Fighters wear helmet, quilted jacket, knee-high boots.

## Materials and colours
- Bronze: very dark patinated brown-green, #242013 in shade up to #6F685C where lit; the wall relief reads near black (#2A261F), faces and metal edges lighter.
- Slabs: light grey-beige granite, #C5C7BA (lit face), #878D8B (shaded face), top #D6D8D3. Smooth, no mortar lines.
- Plaza: large light-grey granite slabs ~#ACABA9 (ortho) / #7D7F7A (photo under cloud), laid in 4-5 m wide bands divided by narrow dark-grey (almost black) granite stripes running toward the monument (04, 01). Round the bronze groups and along the west bed: dark/mid-grey cobble setts #999293 (08, 09, 12), with thin blue-green light strips set in the paving (12).
- Floor plaque ~1 x 0.7 m, black granite, with the dedication text in the cobbles (12), at the sewer group.
- Supreme Court colonnade: columns green, ribbed, ~#738483 (photo 13: green-glass/patina, vertical ribs, dark-metal lettering with unit names on the lower parts); caps with relief rosettes; wall behind blue-black reflective glass. OSM tags these as "marble #deeded", which is wrong in colour (real: green).
- A low red-brick wall with a bronze Kotwica (Polish Underground State anchor) and the inscription "POWSTANIE WARSZAWSKIE 1944" stands beside the square on the Długa side (seen in the Commons set, not kept; the brick wall edge shows at the right of 14_square_panorama.jpg). Brick ~#9C4A3A.

## What stands around
- N / NE: Supreme Court. Its colonnade bays are the OSM building polygons 1496610246..1496610265 (14 m high, "#deeded"): e.g. (-1224,-1710), (-1249,-1705), (-1281,-1718), (-1317,-1764). They form an L: a long wing running SW-NE past the steps (x -1295..-1214) and a short wing running away to the NW.
- W: the street Andersa/Podwale (wide, 4+ lanes, tram) from the ortho: carriageways x -1300..-1250 at z -1700..-1650; the monument is lifted above it by the steps and a grass embankment.
- S/E: the Plac Krasińskich paving ~45 x 45 m at x -1243..-1198, z -1694..-1650, open, no street furniture except bollards on the Długa side and a few trees. Church (Katedra polowa) -1166,-1647 and Kamienica row on Długa at the east edge.
- Beds: juniper (low conifer) beds and a few birch/small trees along the west edge of the plaza (08, 09, 10, ortho), z -1685..-1660.
- No flagpoles seen; no fences. Candles/wreaths on the front step on anniversaries (07).

## Night
- 06 shows warm-white spotlights from the base shining up on the bronze and the slab undersides (golden glow, deep shadows above), glass of the Supreme Court bluish. 07 (1 Aug 2015) shows hundreds of red/orange candle lights and wreaths in front, the bronze lit warm from below.
- Suggested: warm #FFB066 up-lights, 2-3 at the front edge of each group, plus a row of red/orange candle points on the steps on 1 Aug only.

## What OSM gets wrong or misses
- Only the big group has a polygon; the "kanały" group is a nameless 4x5 m way.
- Supreme Court columns tagged marble #deeded, real colour green #738483.
- No steps, paving bands, beds or the manhole are mapped.
- No heights on the monument.
