Files: refs/skyliner/ (6 photos; ortho.jpg 170 m square, frame in ortho.txt, north up). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-03; authors and licences in SOURCES.md. Positions +-5 m. No night photo exists on Commons for it (searched); lit features below are from daylight photos only. Do not confuse with 'Skysawa' (refs/skysawa, a different tower).

## Position (centrum.json)
- Landmark outline 'Skyliner' (25 points): bbox **x -2785..-2727, z 472..550** (the z 532..550 tail is the low east/south annex). Main tower footprint, from the building rows: **x -2785..-2733, z 472..532**, a parallelogram turned ~20 deg from the grid, long sides running NNE-SSW along the rail side; short south face toward the Rondo.
- Building rows: h 170 (mh 23) = the tower shaft; **h 180 (mh 130) at x -2764..-2748, z 474..494** = the crown block (16 x 20 m, at the north end); **h 190 at x -2765..-2762, z 474..478** = the mast (3 m square). Real height 195 m with the mast, 21 m podium of 4-5 storeys.
- Ortho (centre -2760,510; frame x = -2845 + px*0.083, z = 425 + py*0.083): the roof is a dark glass trapezoid leaning ~15 m to the NE of its base (tower perspective); read the footprint from OSM, not the roof. Neighbours in the ortho: the round-ended Crowne Plaza block just east, the 86 m Hub tower above-right (see warsaw_hub.md), Rondo Daszyńskiego top-right.

## Form and height
- **Slanted roof**: the tower is a plain rectangular shaft whose top is cut diagonally, the high edge on the east/Rondo side (photo 1, left tower; photo 3). On the higher corner stands a tall **glass fin / sail** (photo 1, photo 3: a lighter blue vertical plate that rises ~6 storeys above the cut), and a black crown block with the mast at the other end (photos 3, 4: the dark box with an offset notch, mast on its top).
- Shaft ~170 m to the low edge of the cut (OSM 170), crown block to 180, mast to 190; ~42 storeys, so ~3.9-4.0 m per storey.
- The base is 4-5 storeys of glass podium that steps out on the south and west (photo 2 shows the podium lobby glass against the street).

## Facade
- Glass curtain wall, **dark teal-blue glass #2F4A5C** in shade, reflecting sky **#5C86A8** in sun (photos 2, 3); horizontal spandrel bands slightly darker **#26303A** every storey; mullions thin, silver-grey **#9AA5AB**, vertical module ~1.5 m (about 3 mullions per 4.5 m), no big fins. Corners are sharp, no setbacks on the shaft.
- The glass fin on the crown: lighter **#7FA7C8** with a thin silver frame.
- Photo 4 (from the rail side): the cut roof reads as a diagonal line rising to a stepped dark crown; the east face is the widest and flattest.

## Crown, mast and signs
- Crown block: black/charcoal **#1E2226** panels, a square box ~16 x 20 m x 20-25 m high, with a stepped notch on one side (photo 3), mast on top (OSM 190 m, 3 m square, thin steel lattice in photo 3/4).
- **No lit sign or logo** is visible on the top or the crown in any photo (photos 1, 3, 4; looked closely). The game's parody "NOSE AGENCY" sign has no real model. If a sign is kept, the natural face is the **east face of the black crown block** (toward Rondo Daszyński, seen in photo 1 and photo 5); keep it small and unlit-looking by day.
- Roof (ortho): dark glass cut-roof with plant boxes and fan units on the low side, a blue glass/solar strip along the NE edge (the fin's base), the crown box at the north end.

## Base, ground and neighbours
- Podium glass, bike/pedestrian lobby front toward Prosta; a forecourt with granite paving and a few trees (ortho, centre-left). West: Prosta 69 and the newer Wola housing blocks (photo 2 foreground is the old Prosta office block with a skybridge, now removed).
- East: the Crowne Plaza round-ended block (cream cladding, ~86 m), the Hub towers further south-east. South and east of the tower is a building site (crane) in the ortho.
