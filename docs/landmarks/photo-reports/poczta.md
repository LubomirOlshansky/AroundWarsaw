# Poczta Główna (Świętokrzyska 31/33) reference report
Files: refs/poczta/ (4 photos: 1 the building today; 2-3 the pre-war post office in ruins, 1945, history only;
4 is a different building (a low office on a wide street), ignore; ortho.jpg 140 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±4 m.

- Position: OSM w29090521 centre (-923, -138); ortho: the block **x -964..-884, z -162..-77** between
  Świętokrzyska (north, z ~-170) and the street to the south, with **a glazed central hall** (dark pyramidal glass
  roof, x -940..-900, z -140..-115) and a rear courtyard.
- **Corner dome**: a small **round tower with a grey-silver dome** (~6 m across) on the north-east corner at
  about **(-905, -159)**, rising one storey above the roof (1, ortho).
- Front (1): **8 storeys**, pale salmon-pink plaster **#E3C3AE** (shade #C9A994), regular rows of plain windows,
  a cornice under the top storey; the ground floor with a **colonnade of 6 white columns** two storeys high
  framing the entrance under a red "Poczta Polska" band (parody it: POCZTA POLNA or just POCZTA); tall arched
  windows at the corner's ground floor; a recessed white vertical window strip near the entrance.
