# Behind the Eastern Wall stores (Pasaż Wiecha, the pavilions and towers) reference report
Files: refs/dt_pawilony/ (3 photos of Pasaż Wiecha; ortho.jpg 300 m square centred at (-800, 180)); see also
refs/dt_centrum (the stores' fronts). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±4 m.

- The stores (OSM): Junior (-934, 84), Sawa (-891, 203), Wars (-852, 308) along Marszałkowska's east side;
  **Pasaż Wiecha**, the pedestrian street behind them, runs N-S at **x ~-870..-845** from Chmielna (z ~110) to
  Jerozolimskie (z ~330), paved light grey concrete slabs **#C9C8C3**, with **tall white Y-shaped lamp masts**
  (a single white pole splitting into two flat arms with long lamp blades, ~10 m, every ~25 m) along its axis (3),
  black bollards, café gardens with white umbrellas, planters.
- **Pawilon Zodiak**: OSM (-799, 279); ortho: an oval/rounded low pavilion x -830..-790, z 265..295 with a light
  roof (1-2 storeys).
- The **residential towers** east of the pasaż (3: 1960s 20+ storey slabs, grey-white **#D8D8D4** with horizontal
  balcony bands and dark window strips): the long blocks at x -875..-840, z 120..185 and x -845..-770, z 160..225
  in the ortho; heights: use the map's LoD2 (not read here).
- Ground-floor pavilions on the pasaż (3): 2-storey flat-roofed modernist boxes, black/dark-grey lower storey with
  shopfronts, white upper band with a strip window.
- **Ortho 2025-26**: Chmielna (z ~100-120 between x -900 and -780) is dug up for works, and a **building site with
  an open excavation** lies at x -830..-790, z 30..60 (Chmielna / the stores' north end). Decide whether to show
  it (default: no, keep the street).
- Open: building-by-building heights and the Christmas tree on the pasaż (2) for a winter event.
