# Cedet (former Centralny Dom Towarowy / Smyk) reference report

Files: refs/cedet/ (best: 3, 4, 8 corner views 2018-2024, 5 straight-on elevation, 6 night, 7 rebuild structure, ortho.jpg; 1, 2, 9, 10 are the building before 2016). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Ortho frame in SOURCES.md. Positions +-4 m. Written from the photos on F's Mac 2026-11-02.

## Identity
1. A **long glazed 1950s department-store block** with **continuous ribbon windows** in thin bronze-grey frames and **cream stone floor slabs** (rounded edges) between every storey.
2. **Rounded corner(s)**: the SW corner is a smooth quarter-round carrying the same glass bands round it (photos 3, 4).
3. The **crown**: a cream cornice band with fine vertical ribbing at the top of the glazed storeys, then a **set-back glass pavilion** with a flat roof and **two upswept "wing" canopies** (cantilevered roof plates, dark underside) at both ends, like a ski-jump (photos 4, 5).
4. A **tall open ground floor on granite-clad square piers** with shop glass and a slim canopy.
5. The **sculpture on the Jerozolimskie face: a big arrow-tipped loop** (3 loops of steel tube, about 35 m across), grey-green by day, **electric-blue neon** at night. (Replaced the old "SMYK" letters.)

## Position and massing
- Block: x about -520..-440, z 240..326 (ortho: two wings joined by a link): the **south wing** along Aleje Jerozolimskie (south side, trams in the road), x -520..-445, z about 280..326 (79 m x ~35 m, with a **courtyard** hollow in the middle, ortho), and a **north wing** (pentagon/kite roof, x -520..-470, z 240..282, ~50 x 40 m) joined by a recessed link with plant. West of the block is Krucza (the street with trams/cars, rising north), east is the plaza at the head of Bracka with the old Orbis globe building (a 1930s block with a globe on top) at its south end.
- **South wing storeys** (photos 5, 4): ground floor ~6 m (open arcade-like, granite piers 1 x 1 m at ~7 m spacing) + **5 glazed storeys of ~3.7 m** (about 18.5 m) + the **cornice band** (1.5 m) + the **set-back pavilion** (1 storey, ~4 m). Total roughly 30 m (LoD2 23-33 m ✓). Photo 7 (scaffolded) shows ground + 5 floor slabs + roof slab.
- **Crown pavilion**: set back about 5-6 m from the Jerozolimskie edge, covering the middle ~60% of the facade length; flat roof plate with an overhang of ~2 m; at each end the roof plate **tilts up and projects ~6 m beyond the pavilion** (the "wings", photo 5 left and right at the top: dark triangular undersides). Between the wings a lower central roof box.
- **North wing** (the one with the kite-shaped roof): same height, same glazing, same crown; its plan is a pentagon with a long diagonal face toward Bracka (NE) (ortho: sharp point at the east end at about x -452, z 267). Its roof (ortho): a flat roof with a big plant field of round fans (x -505..-480, z 258..268) and a glazed skylight at the SW tip.
- Courtyard and link roofs (ortho): plant units, 4 round roof hoods on the south wing's inner edge (x -498..-476, z 288..292), a glazed lantern.

## Facades (photo 5 straight-on, the Jerozolimskie face)
- **Ground floor**: 11 structural bays (7.2 m) of granite-clad square piers (#B9B2A4) with shop glass between, glass set back ~2 m under the first slab (arcade-like), slim steel canopy, bus/tram stop in front; an entrance at the SW (Krucza corner) and at the centre.
- **Upper floors**: 5 glazed storeys, 11 bays across (matching the piers, each ~7.2 m), **each bay split into 3 window panes** by thin mullions and into 2 sash rows (a transom ~1 m above the slab). The slabs stand out as **cream rounded bands ~0.5 m tall** with a ~0.3 m projection; the glazing recessed 0.4 m behind them; frames bronze-grey, dark.
- **Cornice**: cream band ~1.5 m tall, fine vertical ribs (fluting every ~0.25 m), edge slightly rounded.
- **Krucza face** (west, short ~35 m): same 5 storeys, 4-5 bays; the corner at the Jerozolimskie crossing is the **quarter-round, radius ~10-12 m**; the glass bands continue round it (photos 3, 4, 8). Corner window groups: 3 narrow vertical panes per storey on the curve.
- **Bracka (east) end**: the old picture (photo 8) shows the SE end as a second rounded corner; ortho shows a rounded bevel at the SE. Treat the SE corner as rounded too (radius ~6 m), less certain.

## Roof (ortho and photos)
Grey flat roofs #6A6F76 with plant, 4 round hoods, glazed lantern; crown pavilion roof a pale cream plate #D9D4C8 with dark underside and a few antennas; no masts or signage. The old Orbis globe stands on the neighbour (east), not on Cedet.

## Colours (daylight photos 3, 4, 5)
- Slabs/cornice, sunlit #E6DFCF, shaded #CFC8B8 (cream; earlier report #D9D4C8).
- Frames and mullions: dark bronze-grey #4A4742.
- Glass: dark blue-grey #2E4660, reflecting sky #6F8FB5; the middle storeys read near-black #1F2A36.
- Ground floor piers/granite #B9B2A4 (warm grey), shop glass #28303A, canopy edge #CFC8B8.
- Crown pavilion walls: glass dark #2A3340; roof plates cream #D9D4C8, undersides #3A3A3A.
- Roof #6A6F76. Sculpture by day #6F8478 (patina green-grey), at night #2D5BFF.

## Signs and lettering
Ground-floor tenants with red/white brand fascias (a drugstore chain in red letters, a mobile network in magenta, a pharmacy; names not needed; parody if used). The loop-and-arrow sculpture is a sign of sorts (no letters). The building's own name "CEDET" appears on the hoardings only. Orbis (neighbour) has a vertical "orbis" sign: not Cedet's.

## Night (photo 6)
- The **blue neon loop** (a bright electric blue #2D5BFF, 3 loops and an arrowhead pointing left/west) is the most visible light.
- Floors above are mostly dark glass with scattered warm office lights (#F5C77A) on ~1/3 of the bays; ground-floor shops glow warm white and a red/pink neon sign at the east corner.
- The pavilion crown is dark; the cornice is not lit. Street: tram wires, bright cars, the Palace of Culture floodlit lilac in the distance at the end of the avenue.

## Ground (foot of the building)
- Pavement ~5 m wide of grey granite slabs with a row of young plane/lime trees along Jerozolimskie (x -500..-450, z 330), black bollards at the crossing, tram stop (shelter, glass, ~20 m long, two tracks in the road), a red letterbox and a card/phone kiosk at the SW corner, advertising columns. Krucza side: a wide pavement, a pedestrian crossing at the corner, the new glass office block (north of it, west side: stepped blue-glass, z about 240-280).
- East plaza: Bracka head, parked cars, a couple of kiosks and a market-like stall zone, the Orbis block with a silver globe on its roof.
