# Cedet (Centralny Dom Towarowy, later Smyk) reference report
Files: refs/cedet/ (3 photos: 1 archive 1950s, 2 before the rebuild with the SMYK letters, 3 the rebuilt
Cedet 2024; ortho.jpg 160 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±4 m.

- Position: OSM w238767037 centre (-487, 289); the block **x -520..-440, z 278..326** at the corner of
  **Krucza (west) and Aleje Jerozolimskie (south)**, its rounded corner on the south-west at about (-516, 322),
  facing the Krucza / Jerozolimskie crossing (the start area's crossing). A new angular office block stands north
  of it (x -520..-470, z 240..280).
- Form (3): **5 storeys + a set-back 6th**, ~26 m, long horizontal **ribbon windows** in thin light frames on
  every floor, the corner a smooth **quarter-round** (radius ~12 m) with continuous glass bands; each floor slab
  shows as a thin cream band **#D9D4C8**; glass grey-blue **#8FA3AE**; ground floor taller (~6 m) with shop
  windows and a slim canopy; the roof storey recessed with a light overhanging roof plate (3).
- Before the rebuild (2): the same massing with the corner sign SMYK in colourful letters on the roof (not to
  use: a real brand), ORBIS vertical sign on the next block.
- Night: glass bands lit warm white floor by floor (guess).
