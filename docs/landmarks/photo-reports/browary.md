# Browary Warszawskie: the Central Garden and roofs (K6 check) reference report
Files: refs/browary/ (3 photos: 1 the memorial boulder, 2 aerial during building, 3 the Grzybowska plaza;
ortho.jpg 260 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±3 m.

- **Central Garden**: a raised garden courtyard (on the slab over the Leżakownia) at **x -2479..-2436,
  z -121..-79**, bounded by the long green-roofed slab on the north (x -2475..-2400, z -150..-125), the office wing
  on the east (x -2420..-2400) and lower blocks west and south. It is **densely planted: about 20-25 tree crowns**
  (3-6 m across, young lindens/birches/maples) over a lawn, with **curving gravel paths** in an S shape, white
  café tables and chairs in small clearings, a few dark-blue parasols/benches. The map's 12 trees are too few for
  what the ortho shows (add ~10 more, keep the paths free).
- A tree group on the south-east (x -2440..-2420, z -75..-60) and street trees along Grzybowska.
- **Roofs**: almost every Browary block has a **green sedum roof** (#7C8F55) with planted edges; the north slab's
  roof has a garden with paths and small structures (x -2470..-2410, z -150..-130).
- The Grzybowska plaza (3): dark granite paving in a grid, a long low planter bench wall, young trees in grates.
- The AK memorial boulder (1): granite erratic with a dark plaque and a stone cross, in a flowered bed by the
  entrance (spot not located in the ortho).
