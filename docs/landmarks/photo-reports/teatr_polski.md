# Teatr Polski im. Arnolda Szyfmana (Karasia 2) reference report
Files: refs/teatr_polski/ (4 photos: 1 the corner in winter, 2 and 4 the Karasia front, 3 the stage, inside;
ortho.jpg 140 m square). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574. Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±5 m.

- Position: OSM w61052514 centre (-268, -505); ortho: the theatre's **green copper hipped roof** over the hall at
  about **x -311..-270, z -511..-479**, with lower flat-roofed wings round it; Karasia on its west/north side.
- Form (2, 4): a **neoclassical/early-modern theatre, 3 storeys (~18 m)**, smooth **cream-grey render #E2DDCD**,
  **rounded corner bays (drums) with balustraded tops** at the front's ends, rows of **oval windows (oculi)** on
  the top storey, tall windows with balconettes and a long iron balcony across the middle storey, round-arched
  doors and windows at the ground floor; a stone balustrade along the whole roof edge; the green hall roof
  rising behind.
- A small green kiosk / ticket booth on the walk in front (2).
