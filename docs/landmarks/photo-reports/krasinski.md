# Krasiński Palace (Pałac Krasińskich, Palace of the Commonwealth, Biblioteka Narodowa) reference report
Files: refs/krasinski/ (17 Commons photos 01-17 + ortho.jpg 3072x3072, SOURCES.md has licences).
Game coords: x=(lon-21.024)*68240.4, z=(52.234-lat)*110574. Ortho: x = -1510 + px/7.314, z = -1900 + py/7.314. Ortho numbers are eyeballed, +-3 m; the building leans in the ortho (roof shifted a few metres SE of the footprint). Compass: bearing clockwise from north; game direction for bearing b: dx = sin b, dz = -cos b.

## Orientation and size
- OSM way 34614532 (name Pałac Krasińskich, building:levels 3, height 18, roof #666666 concrete, colour #dde0c8): bbox x -1408..-1341, z -1727..-1647, centre (-1376,-1685), 80-node polygon. Minimum rectangle: 77.6 m long x 29.7 m deep, long axis running NNW-SSE at bearing ~150/330 deg (30 deg off N-S, not NE-SW as the brief guessed: in game x/z the long side goes from (-1384,-1727) to (-1364,-1647)).
- The **street (east) front faces ENE, bearing ~60 deg**, toward Plac Krasińskich, Andersa street, and the Supreme Court. The **garden (west) front faces WSW, bearing ~240 deg**, onto Ogród Krasińskich. Game outward directions: east front (+0.87,-0.5), garden front (-0.87,+0.5).
- End wings: NNW end at (-1384..-1408, -1727..-1698) with two projecting corner pavilions (pyramid hip roofs, ortho (-1397,-1713), (-1388,-1722)); SSE end at (-1364..-1341, -1647..-1664), shorter, with the stair/end bay.
- The real east face is flush except for a **central projecting risalit ~3 m**: OSM shows steps (-1360,-1693)..(-1350,-1676), the risalit in the middle of the east face, about 22 m wide.
- Heights (LoD2 as given): eaves ~16.5 m, ridge ~21.5 m; photos: three main storeys, then attic/roof. Pediment apex ~22 m, apex statue to ~28 m, chimneys to ~24 m. OSM's 18 m is between eaves and ridge: OK as an average.

## Colours (sampled from the photos, light sunny day)
- Walls: warm white plaster **#E0E1E3** (sunny: #F0EFEA, shaded: #C9C9C4). The OSM #dde0c8 is a bit too yellow; photos say white-with-grey.
- Trim, pilasters, window frames, balustrades, cornices, pediment: pale grey stone **#C3C5BC**, in shade #A9ABA6. Pediment relief and statues: stone, **#B9BBBA** (lit up to #D8D8D4).
- Roof: copper patina, pale mint green **#A9CDB4** (photo 11, lit slope; #ADD4B9 / #AED6BF on other slopes); in the ortho the lit slopes come out #B5C8BE; ridge/hips slightly darker #8FB5A0 (shade). Not grey #666666 as OSM says. Roof is flat-seamed standing-seam copper (fine vertical lines, ~0.5 m).
- Flat lead/tar-grey walkways behind the east-side attic balustrade: **#A0A7AC** (ortho), strip ~3 m wide.
- Chimneys: white-grey plastered boxes with a stone cap, ~#E5E5E0 (photo 11), ~1.2 x 1.2 m, 3 m tall.
- Ground floor of the street front: pale cream rustication; wings' ground arches: dark navy glazed #041830; the entrance doors: dark wood, carved, #6B4A32.
- Garden bed colours: round parterre flowers red/pink and yellow, box hedge dark green; palace-front lawn #A0997E (dry, ortho) / grass green #6E8F4A (photos); gravel paths #D5CDB9; lawn at the east: dry meadow.

## Street (east) front: toward Plac Krasińskich (photos 01-08)
- Layout bottom to top: a high plinth/ground storey (low rusticated base with square windows in the wings and a loggia/arched ground storey), the piano nobile (tall windows with segmental and triangular pediments), a shorter top storey with square windows under a cornice, then the roof.
- **Central risalit**: 5 window axes (photos 02, 06 count 5 at the piano nobile: 2+1 centre+2), ~20 m wide, framed by 6 pilasters running through the two upper storeys, slightly projecting. Ground floor of the risalit: the main entrance with a 2-column porch-balcony (balustraded balcony on 2 pairs of pilasters, photo 02), a pair of dark carved doors with a stone portal, "Biblioteka Narodowa" lettering over it, 3-4 steps up from a low terrace (lawn in front, hedge each side). Above the entrance the central window is arched (round-headed fan window) with a cartouche (02, 08): a royal monogram with two winged putti, a crown with cherubs on top.
- **Pediment**: triangular, spanning the whole risalit, **sculpted** with a high relief frieze (Roman triumph: a chariot with horses, soldiers, trophies, figures in antique armour), stone colour #B9BBBA, moulded cornice, tympanum recessed ~0.5 m. On the apex a tall statue (a standing antique figure, ~4 m) on a pedestal, flanked by trophy groups; on each pediment foot (above the risalit end pilasters) a standing winged figure/angel ~2.5 m with a raised arm, on a plinth (photos 06, 07). So **statues**: 1 apex + 2 flanking groups at apex + 2 foot statues ~ 5 pieces; 4 more on the roof ridge line of the wings in the garden front (photo 11 shows a tall statue left of the pediment on the roof at x 520, and one right at x 1050).
- **Wings**: on each side of the risalit, upper-floor open **loggia/arcade of 3-4 round arches** (balustraded, photos 01, 03, 09, 04: 4 arches on each side in 01/03) over the ground storey; further out the wing walls are plain with square windows and balustrade at roof edge (a stone balustrade with vases runs along the whole east roof edge: 03, 14, 05). Ground floor of the wings: arched blue windows (04, 10) and square windows, an arcade of pointed-top blind niches on the garden end.
- **Corner risalits/pavilions**: the NNW end has two stepped pavilions projecting toward the garden side (ortho), the SSE end a corner bay; they have pyramid hip roofs and pilaster strips, rusticated corner quoins, ~7 x 8 m each. Window bays per full east face: roughly 15-17 axes (5 in the risalit + 5-6 each side, my count from 01/03; the OSM polygon implies 16).
- Attic balustrade: stone balustrade with vases/urns above the main cornice on the whole east face, one vase every ~3 m.

## Garden (west) front (photos 09-12, 16)
- Central risalit identical in form (5 axes, pediment with relief, apex statue, 2 foot statues, 6 chimneys along the roofline: count 5 visible in photo 11 at x 515, 1000, 1200, 1400, 1490). Ground floor has a **pair of side arcade/blind arches in blue/navy** at the SSE end (11: 4 arches on the right wing) and a plain door with a small balustraded balcony in the centre (the garden entrance, 2 pilaster columns, glazed double door).
- Roof detail (11, 12): copper hip roof, low pitch ~25 deg, 10-12 small dark dormer louvres in a row below the eaves, chimneys behind the pediment, the pediment cut into the eaves line.
- In front: round flower bed with a fountain (photos 10, 12; ortho: a circle ~16 m diameter at (-1410,-1670) with a centre basin ~5 m, red/orange flower rings, dark round shrubs ring r=7 m), cut-out box-hedge beds, wide gravel avenue and terrace (ortho x -1440..-1420, z -1770..-1650), and a 5 m wide path along the facade. Further west: pond with a sculpture in the middle (13) at about (-1437,-1660), and a second parterre (small hedge lozenge at (-1428,-1646)).

## Roof plan (ortho, photos 16, 17)
- Green copper roof, ~78 x 24 m. Main ridge runs along the long axis; two transverse gables at the risalits (the pediments cut the eaves on both faces); hip ends at the NNW and SSE ends; separate pyramid hips over the NNW corner pavilions. Ridge runs along the axis from (-1395,-1712) to (-1362,-1660).
- Flat grey strips (lead, #A0A7AC) along both long eaves behind balustrades, ~2.5-3 m wide, the east strip is the wider one (ortho).
- Dormers/hatches: small dark triangular dormers, ~3-4 on each long slope of each half (about 12 per slope in total in the louvre row seen in photo 11); 5-6 small rectangular hatches on the ridge (ortho (-1387,-1689), (-1384,-1690), (-1363,-1668), (-1360,-1670), ...).
- Chimneys: 5-6 along each face roofline, 1.2 m boxes (photo 11 shows them at the garden side).

## What stands in front
- East: a wide lawn ~35 m deep with a worn diagonal path (ortho (-1352,-1740)..(-1350,-1690)), a gravel promenade along the facade (~5 m, ortho), a narrow low stone/iron barrier at the pavement edge; the Andersa/Podwale carriageway beyond (4-5 lanes), then Plac Krasińskich; no fence on the east side (01, 03). Street lamps (tall, 2-arm, dark green) and low bollards at the pavement (04).
- Small kiosk/utility block at (-1347,-1690) on the lawn (ortho dark rectangle), a low brick shed at (-1360,-1743).
- Fence and gates: iron fence, tall black bars with gilt tips on a low stone plinth, on the north side at (-1360,-1745) gate (photo 15). (Other gates of the park were not checked.)
- Garden side: Ogród Krasińskich, the park: terrace and 6+ steps from the palace door, parterre, pond W of the parterre at about (-1440,-1655), mature trees (willows at the pond).

## What OSM gets wrong
- Roof tagged concrete #666666; real is green copper.
- building colour #dde0c8 is yellowish; real is white #E0E1E3.
- Orientation: the building runs NNW-SSE (my brief said NE-SW); OSM matches that (correct).
- Footprint merges the corner pavilions; the central risalit step on the east face exists in OSM and is real; the NNW end has a notch (-1388,-1718)..(-1399,-1712) to the pavilions.
- No height split: OSM 18 m; LoD2 16.5 eaves, 21.5 ridge is more accurate. OSM lists no statues; apex statue ~28 m total.
- OSM nodes 2472050481/-505/-510 (statues, height 6) at about (-1289,-1790) and fountain 460050271 at (-1277,-1773) are 60-100 m NE of the palace by the Supreme Court garden wall; I did not identify them.
