# Pomnik Jana Kilińskiego reference report
Files: refs/kilinski/ (4 photos, ortho.jpg 100 m square centred on OSM). Game coords: x = (lon - 21.024) * 68240.4, z = (52.234 - lat) * 110574 (+x east, +z south). Gathered on F's Mac 2026-11-02; licences in SOURCES.md; ortho frame in ortho.txt (north up). Positions ±3 m.

- Position: OSM w404216456 **(-896, -1573)**, ul. Podwale at Piekarska, on a small paved plaza between Podwale's
  houses and the city walls' lawn (ortho: the plinth casts a shadow north-west; café pavilions along the walls'
  edge to the east).
- Form (1-3): **dark granite pedestal ~2 x 2 m, ~4.5 m tall** (polished dark grey-black **#2D2D2F**, a plaque on the
  front) on a two-step base; on it Kiliński striding, **sabre raised high in the right hand**, the left at his
  belt, in a long kontusz coat and fur cap: dark bronze **#35362F**, ~3.5 m. Total ~8 m.
- Faces **south-west, along Podwale** (the cream Podwale houses and the green/orange Old Town houses behind).
