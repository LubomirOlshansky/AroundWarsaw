## Outline and corners (OSM, apartments, no name, h=14.0 = a default; centrum.json)
- 63 points, a **sawtooth slab ~64 x 12-14 m**, long axis running from the NE end (-778, -1148) to the SW end (-826, -1190), bearing about 38 deg south of west.
  Each step in the outline is ~3-4 m long with a 1-1.5 m jog (the balcony boxes).
- NE end: (-779.0, -1147.5) - (-789.1, -1143.4) (north corner) and the NE short side to (-776.1, -1149.6).
- NW long side (towards Moliera, the stepped terrace face): (-789.5, -1144.2) - (-791.4, -1149.0) - (-795.3, -1152.5) - (-799.7, -1156.8) - (-803.2, -1162.3) - (-807.1, -1165.7) - (-811.9, -1168.5) - (-816.8, -1174.6) - (-820.6, -1178.1) - (-825.9, -1178.5) - (-832.7, -1189.3) (SW corner).
- SE long side (the courtyard face with the entrance): (-776.1, -1149.6) - (-781.9, -1163.6) - (-788.0, -1166.3) - (-794.1, -1169.0) - (-799.2, -1172.0) - (-803.7, -1180.4) - (-809.7, -1183.2) - (-814.8, -1186.3) - (-817.5, -1190.4) - (-819.2, -1194.7) (south corner).
  SW end: (-832.7, -1189.3) - (-828.6, -1190.9) - (-819.2, -1194.7).
- Centre ~ (-805, -1174), matches the OSM address point for Kozia 9 (-804.5, -1169). LoD2 footprint 3105 (54 pts) is the same within ~1 m.
- Ortho: a diagonal slab of 5-6 grey roof terraces stepping down to the SW, a garden/courtyard of dense trees to the north-east, a car park and
  the Moliera slab (grey roof, diamond-textured facade) to the north-west, the rows of old Kozia tenements (red tile roofs) to the east. The road
  names near it: Moliera ~69 m, Kozia ~85 m.

## Heights (LoD2, map y = metres above sea level - 100)
- Ground y = 7.5 (the base level), top levels along the axis (t from the NE end): 0-8 m: 31.2; **8-20 m: 35.5** (the tall end with the rounded stair tower);
  20-44 m: 31.4 / 28.3 / 25.7 steps; 44-60 m: 25.9 -> 24.0; the low SW end 23.1.
  So about **28 m (8 floors) at the tall NE end** (35.5 - 7.5), ~24 m in the middle, ~16 m (5 floors) at the SW end. OSM's 14 m is wrong, use LoD2.
- Floor height ~3.1 m. Photo 1: 8 floors at the tall end, 5 at the low end, a basement garage ramp at the NE end.

## Facade, colours and rhythm (photos 1, 2)
- Cream-grey roughcast concrete (stained darker in streaks): sunlit **#B8B1A0**, shade **#8F897A**; window frames white **#EEEEEA** (narrow strip windows), underside of
  the boxes and the recesses darker **#7A7468**; the entrance doors warm wood **#8C5A32**; the car-park retaining walls grey **#8E8E8C**; planters dark green.
- Plan module ~3.3 m; per module one box on every floor, the boxes **staggered** so the profile in plan is a saw-tooth and the roofline steps one bay at a time
  (photo 1: the steps are one floor each, every 2-3 bays). Photo 2 shows the boxes as cantilevered slabs with a parapet, like a stepped hillside.
- Tall NE end: a **half-round stair tower** (tallest element), the vertical fin pattern of the long wall at the NW (Moliera) face.

## Facts to confirm
- Year 1974-76 (brief; photo 2's Commons title says 1976). Architects Kuźmienko & Sembrat (brief; not verified from a source).
- The brief says "brick terraces": **the photos show cream-grey roughcast concrete, not brick** (the brick-coloured one is Karowa 18a).
- The five stepped sections: LoD2 shows ~5 roof levels (35.5, 31.4, 28.3, 25.7, 23.1), matching "five".
- Whether the NW face on Moliera has the same boxes as the SE face (photo 1 shows the SE side; photo 2 the other side, probably NW): check on the Mac.
