## What is trending (evidence)
- Japanese comfort food, matcha, onigiri (Konbini, udon bars); Polish food reimagined (pierogi with kompot);
  wine bars with bubbles, Italian aperitivo, Scandinavian bakeries, Thai street food, tacos, ice cream with caviar.
- Pop-ups "lasting as long as a New Year's resolution", secret cocktail bars hidden in alleys, rooftop bars
  that close in winter, concept stores (shop + gallery + café), speciality coffee, vintage markets, "limited" everything.
- Internet 2025: Dubai (pistachio-kunafa) chocolate queues, matcha everything, Labubu fanged plush, padel boom.
- City: new e-scooter rules from 3 June 2026 (12 km/h on Złota, Zgody, Bracka, Plac Centralny; parking and
  riding bans in Park Sowińskiego; under-16 helmets), cycling culture, M2/M3 metro works.
