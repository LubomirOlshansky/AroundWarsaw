# Tiny Warsaw and Around Warsaw: what we learn (2026-11-04)

F shared tinywarsaw.app (its landmark gallery) as a project they like. We read it as
inspiration only: nothing is copied, downloaded or re-used from it; these are lessons to do in
our own way. It is a free hobby 3D map of Warsaw in an autumn low-poly look (drive, bus,
balloon, plane, online, day/night, live weather), built from the same open data as ours
(OpenStreetMap, GUGiK 2025, plus ZTM stops, the city's tree crown map, Open-Meteo).

**What this is based on, and what it is not.** The gallery page lists its models by name and
height. Its 3D thumbnails and viewer need WebGL, which the cloud container cannot render, so
the model *look* (colour, detail, silhouette) was not seen here. The look lessons below are
therefore left as a Mac/F task (section 3). Everything in sections 1 and 2 is read from the
gallery list (1,393 entries) and the page text.

## 1. What they chose to model (from the list)

- **Volume over fame.** 1,393 "landmark" models, but only a few dozen are famous. About 244 are
  metro station entrances (named "Metro <station> · n"), 75+ are the Filtry waterworks, and ~58
  are airport sheds and hangars. About 160 names are raw OSM ids ("Airport hangar 80858583"),
  so a large part of the gallery is generated per OSM building by use, not hand-modelled.
- **Infrastructure counts as landmarks.** Tunnels under al. Niepodległości and Marszałkowska,
  five footbridges over Wisłostrada, Rondo Czterdziestolatka as "tram diamond + twin flyovers"
  (listed as 310 m and 232 m), Warszawa Wschodnia underpass, a car-park ramp by PKiN.
- **Whole blocks, not only icons.** Osiedle Muranów Południowy by pierzeja, Plac Zbawiciela
  fronts (MDM), MDM Plac Konstytucji (7 buildings), Osiedle Batorego, "PWSM punktowiec 1967"
  point blocks along Jerozolimskie, Spiska, Niemcewicza, Grójecka. Names carry the year and the
  architect's scheme, i.e. the model is tied to a story.
- **Towers are modelled with real heights** (Varso 310 m, PKiN 225, Warsaw Unit 202, Q22 195,
  Skyliner 194, Złota 44 189, Spire 187, WTT 184). Matches our own list.

## 2. Against our approach

| | Tiny Warsaw (from the list) | Around Warsaw |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source of detail | per-building generation by OSM use, plus hand-made landmarks | photo-built landmarks only from Mac-gathered photos (model-from-photos) |
| Breadth | 1,393 named models across the city | a ranked inventory (docs/CITY_INVENTORY.md), centre first |
| Modernism | named by scheme and year (PWSM 1967, MDM, Muranów) | "Betonowy album" quest (docs/design/modernism-collection.md) |
| Infrastructure | tunnels, footbridges, flyovers are gallery items | bridges and ramps done as map work, not as named, findable models |

## 3. Lessons to apply (short, concrete)

1. **A "scheme" label beats a lone address.** Modernism blocks read as a family: name them by
   scheme and year (PWSM 1967 point blocks, MDM, Muranów pierzeje) and build one shared kit
   per scheme. Add the PWSM point blocks (Jerozolimskie 135-141, Niemcewicza 24-25, Spiska
   4A/16A, Grójecka 14A) to the modernism collection as a kit candidate: one model, ten places.
   (Candidate only: needs photos from the Mac first, per the photos-only rule.)
2. **Name infrastructure like landmarks.** Footbridges over Wisłostrada, the Marszałkowska and
   Niepodległości tunnels and the Czterdziestolatka roundabout are findable places there. Our
   CITY_INVENTORY can rank them (a short "infrastructure" tier) so street passes cover them.
3. **Generate the long tail by OSM use, hand-build the head.** Their ratio (a few dozen
   hand-made, a thousand generated by use) matches our shared-kit direction. Keep spending
   photo effort only on the ranked head; for the tail use kits keyed by building use.
4. **Gallery as a QA tool.** A filterable list of every model with its height is a cheap way
   to spot gaps and duplicates. The city console (thread "Build city progress console") can
   carry such a list from our `sites` data.
5. **Look lessons: not learned yet.** Colour palette, silhouette simplification and how
   landmarks read small must be judged from screenshots. Ask F for 4-8 gallery screenshots, or
   run the Mac browser, then add them here. Do not store or commit their images.
