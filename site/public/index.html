About the author

Hi, I'm Lubomir Olshansky. By day I build iOS and Kotlin Multiplatform apps at a bank in Poland, and I've lived in Warsaw for twelve years.

As a kid I dreamed of a game set in the city I live in. When I saw Isometric Warsaw and what generative AI makes possible, it finally looked doable. I want it to hold what living here is like, not just the map.
