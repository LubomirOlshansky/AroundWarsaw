The real centre, block by block

About 21 km² of central Warsaw, from Arkadia to Stadion Narodowy and from MDM to the Old Town. Streets, bridges and trees come from OpenStreetMap and Poland's surveying office; the landmarks are built by hand.

Missions with a Warsaw accent

Everything happens at real addresses, and the city talks back: Polish first, English underneath.

Kebab at 3 a.m.

A stand hands you an order for a real address. The kebab cools as you drive and the tip cools with it. At night a courier on a bike may race you to the door: he takes the parks and the crossings, you take the road.

Rescue calls

Someone is stuck on a real roof, a real square or a sandbank of the Vistula. Get there by car or by helicopter, pick them up and race the clock to a real hospital. The police may even escort you.

The Leżak round-up

Rental e-scooters lie all over the pavements at night. Collect ten of them, five fit in the boot at a time. Gently: under 15 km/h, or they won't get in.

The gentle police

Speed traps, the lizak and the city guard on the promenades. Never a fine, never a game over: a talking-to, a wheel clamp for ten seconds, and a chase that ends when the shift does.

The city notebook

MOJA WARSZAWA: 72 real places in nine chapters, from techno cellars to the river steps and the milk bars. Find one and the game writes it down, with a true fact and a local joke.

  1. LusterkoNight and techno

    Otwiera się, gdy inni zamykają. W niedzielę o dziewiątej też.

    It opens when the rest close. At nine on a Sunday, too.

    The palm at Rondo de Gaulle'a has stood here since 2002.

  2. Plac ZbawicielaQueueing for the trend

    Przychodzi się tu zobaczyć, kto przyszedł się pokazać.

    You come here to see who came here to be seen.

    The MDM blocks round the square were built in 1950–1952.

  3. Bar PyzowyFood

    O trzynastej w kolejce stoi student, emeryt i prezes. Wszyscy po pyzy.

    At one p.m. the queue holds a student, a pensioner and a CEO. All for dumplings.

    Warsaw's first milk bar opened in 1896.

  4. Plac KonstytucjiRetro and chic

    Piłka wisi w powietrzu od sześćdziesięciu lat. Nikt jej nie odbił.

    The ball's been in the air for sixty years. Nobody has returned it.

    The volleyball-player neon here dates from 1961.

About the author

Hi, I'm Lubomir Olshansky. By day I'm a mobile developer, building iOS and Kotlin Multiplatform apps at a bank in Poland. I've lived in Warsaw for twelve years.

As a kid I dreamed of playing a game set in the city I lived in: my streets, my tram stops, the places I actually knew. For years that stayed a dream. Then I saw Isometric Warsaw, a pixel-art map of the city built from open data, and what generative AI now lets one person build, and it suddenly looked doable.

So I'm making it. Not just a map of Warsaw, but what it's like to live here: the kebab at 3 a.m., the queue outside the club at dawn, mum's jars on a Sunday, the city guard reminding you it's a promenade. If you live here too, tell me what's missing.
